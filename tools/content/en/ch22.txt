At that time, Śākyamuni Buddha arose from the Dharma seat, manifested great spiritual powers, and with his right hand rubbed the crowns of immeasurable Bodhisattva Mahāsattvas, speaking these words:
"Throughout immeasurable hundreds of thousands of millions of incalculable kalpas, I cultivated this difficult-to-attain Dharma of anuttarasamyaksambodhi; I now entrust it to you all. You should single-mindedly propagate this Dharma, widely causing it to increase."
In this manner three times he rubbed the crowns of the Bodhisattva Mahāsattvas, speaking these words: "Throughout immeasurable hundreds of thousands of millions of incalculable kalpas, I cultivated this difficult-to-attain Dharma of anuttarasamyaksambodhi; I now entrust it to you all. You should receive, uphold, read, recite, and widely proclaim this Dharma, causing all wandering beings universally to hear and know it.
For what reason? The Tathāgata possesses great love and compassion, free of stinginess, and free of fear; he is able to give to wandering beings the Buddha's wisdom, the Tathāgata's wisdom, and spontaneous wisdom; the Tathāgata is the great donor to all wandering beings. You too should follow and learn the Tathāgata's Dharma, not giving rise to stinginess.
In future ages, if there is a good man or good woman who believes in the Tathāgata's wisdom, you should expound this Lotus Sūtra for them, causing them to hear and know it, in order to cause that person to attain the Buddha's wisdom. If there are wandering beings who do not believe and accept it, you should instruct, benefit, and delight them within other deep dharmas of the Tathāgata. If you can act like this, you will have repaid the kindness of all Buddhas."
At that time, the Bodhisattva Mahāsattvas, hearing the Buddha speak thus, were all filled with great joy pervading their bodies; with doubled reverence, bowing, lowering their heads, and joining their palms toward the Buddha, they simultaneously raised their voices, saying:
"As the World-Honored One commands, we will fully practice it. Yes, World-Honored One! We pray you will have no anxiety."
The assembly of Bodhisattva Mahāsattvas, in this manner three times, simultaneously raised their voices, saying: "As the World-Honored One commands, we will fully practice it. Yes, World-Honored One! We pray you will have no anxiety."
Then Śākyamuni Buddha caused all the emanation Buddhas who had arrived from the ten directions to return each to their native lands, speaking these words: "May the Buddhas each dwell in peace as they please; and the stūpa of Prabhūtaratna Buddha may return as before."
When these words were spoken, the immeasurable emanation Buddhas of the ten directions seated upon lion seats beneath jewel trees, Prabhūtaratna Buddha, Viśiṣṭacāritra and the boundless incalculable assembly of Bodhisattvas, Śāriputra and the fourfold assembly of Śrāvakas, as well as all devas, humans, and asuras in the world, hearing what the Buddha expounded, all rejoiced greatly.
