At that time, Bhaiṣajyarāja Bodhisattva arose from his seat, bared his right shoulder, joined his palms toward the Buddha, and addressed the Buddha: "World-Honored One! If a good man or good woman is able to receive and uphold the Lotus Sūtra—whether reading, reciting fluently, or copying the scriptural scrolls—how much merit do they attain?"
The Buddha addressed Bhaiṣajyarāja: "If a good man or good woman makes offerings to Buddhas equal to eight hundred billion inconceivable numbers of Ganges sands: what do you think: is the merit attained plentiful or not?"
"Extremely plentiful, World-Honored One."
The Buddha said: "If a good man or good woman, regarding this scripture, can receive and uphold even a single four-line gāthā, reading, reciting, understanding its meaning, and practicing as taught, their merit is exceedingly plentiful."
At that time, Bhaiṣajyarāja Bodhisattva addressed the Buddha: "World-Honored One! I will now bestow a dhāraṇī mantra upon expounders of the Dharma to protect them."
Immediately he spoke the mantra:
ANYE MANYE MANE MAMANE CIRE CARITE ŚAME ŚAMITĀ VIŚĀNTE MUKTE MUKTATAME ŚAME AVIṢAME SAMASAME JAYE KṢAYE KṢĪṆE ŚĀNTE ŚAMITE DHĀRAṆĪ ĀLOKABHĀSE PRATYAVEKṢAṆI NIVIṢṬE ABHYANTARANIVIṢṬE ATYANTAPĀRIŚUDDHI UTKULE MUKULE ARALE PARABALE ŚUKLĀVAKṢE ASAMASAME BUDDHAVILOKITE DHARMAPARĪKṢITE SAṄGHANIRGHOṢAṆI NIRGHOṢA NIRGHOṢI MANTRE MANTRĀKṢAYATE RUTE RUTAKAUŚALYE AKṢAYE AKṢAYATĀYĀṂ AVALO AMĀNYANATĀYA SVĀHĀ
"World-Honored One! This dhāraṇī divine mantra was spoken by Buddhas equal to sixty-two billion Ganges sands. If someone invades or defames this Dharma teacher, they have invaded and defamed these Buddhas."
Then Śākyamuni Buddha praised Bhaiṣajyarāja Bodhisattva: "Excellent, excellent, Bhaiṣajyarāja! That out of compassion to protect this Dharma teacher you speak this dhāraṇī, bringing abundant benefit to wandering beings."
At that time, Pradānaśūra Bodhisattva addressed the Buddha: "World-Honored One! I too, to protect those who read, recite, receive, and uphold the Lotus Sūtra, will speak a dhāraṇī. If this Dharma teacher attains this dhāraṇī, whether yakṣas, rākṣasas, pūtanas, kṛtyas, kumbhāṇḍas, or hungry ghosts seek their shortcomings, none will gain an opening."
Immediately before the Buddha he spoke the mantra:
JVALE MAHĀJVALE UKKE TUKKE MUKKE AḌE AḌĀVATI NṚTYE NṚTYĀVATI IṬṬINI VIṬṬINI CIṬṬINI NṚTYĀNI NṚTYĀVATI SVĀHĀ
"World-Honored One! This dhāraṇī divine mantra was spoken by Buddhas equal to the sands of the Ganges, who all rejoice in accord. If someone invades or defames this Dharma teacher, they have invaded and defamed these Buddhas."
At that time, Vaiśravaṇa Heavenly King, protector of the world, addressed the Buddha: "World-Honored One! I too, out of compassion for wandering beings and to protect this Dharma teacher, will speak this dhāraṇī."
Immediately he spoke the mantra:
AṬṬE TAṬṬE NAṬṬE VANAṬṬE ANAḌE NĀḌI KUNAḌI SVĀHĀ
"World-Honored One! With this divine mantra I protect the Dharma teacher; I myself will also protect those who uphold this scripture, causing them within a hundred yojanas to be free of all misfortunes and decline."
At that time, Dhṛtarāṣṭra Heavenly King was present in this assembly, reverently surrounded by tens of billions of inconceivable numbers of gandharvas; proceeding before the Buddha, he joined his palms and addressed the Buddha: "World-Honored One! I too, with a dhāraṇī divine mantra, will protect those who uphold the Lotus Sūtra."
Immediately he spoke the mantra:
AGAṆE GAṆE GAURĪ GANDHĀRI CAṆḌĀLI MĀTAṄGĪ PUKKASĪ SAMAŚI SVASTI SVĀHĀ
"World-Honored One! This dhāraṇī divine mantra was spoken by forty-two billion Buddhas. If someone invades or defames this Dharma teacher, they have invaded and defamed these Buddhas."
At that time, there were rākṣasīs: the first named Lambā, the second named Vilambā, the third named Kūṭadantī, the fourth named Puṣpadantī, the fifth named Kṛṣṇadantī, the sixth named Bahukeśī, the seventh named Anivartitā, the eighth named Mālādhārī, the ninth named Kuntī, and the tenth named Sarvasattvojohārī.
These ten rākṣasīs, together with Hārītī and her children and attendants, went to where the Buddha was, and with one voice addressed the Buddha:
"World-Honored One! We too wish to protect those who read, recite, receive, and uphold the Lotus Sūtra, eliminating their misfortunes. If someone seeks the Dharma teacher's shortcomings, we will cause them not to gain an opening."
Immediately before the Buddha they spoke the mantra:
ITI ME ITI ME ITI ME ITI ME ITI ME NIME NIME NIME NIME NIME RUHE RUHE RUHE RUHE RUHE STUHE STUHE STUHE STUHE STUHE SVĀHĀ
"Rather climb upon my head than torment the Dharma teacher!
Whether yakṣas, rākṣasas, hungry ghosts, pūtanas, kṛtyas, vetālas, skandas, unmādas, apasmāras, yakṣa-kṛtyas, or human-kṛtyas;
Whether fevers of one day, two days, three days, four days, up to seven days, or constant fevers;
Whether in male form, female form, boy form, or girl form, down to within dreams—none will torment them!"
Immediately before the Buddha they spoke gāthās:
If one disobeys our mantra,
Disturbing the expounder of Dharma,
Their head will break into seven pieces,
Like the branches of the arjaka tree.
Like the sin of killing parents,
Or the misfortune of pressing oil,
Deceiving people with scales and measures,
Or Devadatta's sin of breaking the Saṅgha:
Those who offend this Dharma teacher,
Will incur misfortunes such as these!
The rākṣasīs, having spoken these gāthās, addressed the Buddha: "World-Honored One! We ourselves will personally protect those who receive, uphold, read, recite, and practice this scripture, causing them to attain peace and security, free of misfortunes, dispelling all poisons."
The Buddha addressed the rākṣasīs: "Excellent, excellent! That you can protect even those who uphold merely the name of the Lotus, your blessings are beyond measure; how much more so protecting those who fully receive and uphold it, making offerings to the scriptural scrolls—flowers, incense, necklaces, powdered incense, paste incense, burning incense, banners, canopies, music; lighting various lamps: ghee lamps, oil lamps, scented oil lamps, sumanā flower oil lamps, campaka flower oil lamps, vārṣikā flower oil lamps, and utpala flower oil lamps, hundreds of thousands of kinds of offerings like these! Kuntī! You and your attendants should protect such Dharma teachers as these."
When this Dhāraṇī Chapter was expounded, sixty-eight thousand people attained the forbearance of non-arising dharmas.
