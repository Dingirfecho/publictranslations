At that time, Bodhisattva Mahāsattvas equal to the dust motes of a thousand worlds who had welled forth from the earth, all before the Buddha single-mindedly joined their palms, gazed upon the venerable countenance, and addressed the Buddha:
"World-Honored One! After the Buddha's extinction, in the lands where the World-Honored One's emanation bodies dwell and in the places where they pass into extinction, we will widely expound this scripture. For what reason? We ourselves wish to attain this genuine, pure, great Dharma, to receive, uphold, read, recite, explain, copy, and make offerings to it."
Then the World-Honored One, before Mañjuśrī and immeasurable hundreds of thousands of millions of Bodhisattva Mahāsattvas who had long dwelled in the Sahā world, as well as bhikṣus, bhikṣuṇīs, upāsakas, upāsikās, devas, Nagas, yakṣas, gandharvas, asuras, garuḍas, kiṃnaras, mahoragas, humans, and non-humans—before the entire assembly, manifested great spiritual powers: extending his broad and long tongue upward to the Brahmā Heaven, and from all pores emitted light of immeasurable, countless colors, universally illuminating worlds of the ten directions.
The Buddhas seated upon lion seats beneath the jewel trees also did likewise, extending broad and long tongues and emitting immeasurable light.
When Śākyamuni Buddha and the Buddhas beneath the jewel trees manifested their spiritual powers, a full hundred thousand years elapsed, after which they drew back their tongues. Simultaneously they cleared their throats and snapped their fingers together; these two sounds pervaded worlds of the Buddhas in the ten directions, and the earth shook in six ways.
The wandering beings within them—devas, Nagas, yakṣas, gandharvas, asuras, garuḍas, kiṃnaras, mahoragas, humans, and non-humans—through the Buddha's spiritual power, all saw the Buddhas seated upon lion seats beneath immeasurable, boundless hundreds of thousands of millions of jewel trees in this Sahā world; and saw Śākyamuni Buddha together with Prabhūtaratna Tathāgata seated upon the lion seat within the jewel stūpa; and saw immeasurable, boundless hundreds of thousands of millions of Bodhisattva Mahāsattvas and the fourfold assembly reverently surrounding Śākyamuni Buddha.
Having seen this, they all rejoiced greatly, attaining what they had never had before.
Immediately the devas in open space proclaimed in loud voices: "Across these immeasurable, boundless hundreds of thousands of millions of incalculable worlds, there is a land named Sahā; within it is a Buddha named Śākyamuni, who now for the sake of Bodhisattva Mahāsattvas expounds the Great Vehicle sūtra named the Lotus Flower of the Wonderful Dharma, a teaching for Bodhisattvas and guarded in the mindfulness of the Buddhas. You should rejoice in accord with a deep mind, and should bow down and make offerings to Śākyamuni Buddha!"
All those wandering beings, hearing the voices in open space, joined their palms toward the Sahā world and said: "Homage to Śākyamuni Buddha! Homage to Śākyamuni Buddha!"
With various flowers, incense, necklaces, banners, canopies, personal ornaments, and rare treasures, they all scattered them together toward the Sahā world from afar. The scattered objects, arriving from the ten directions like gathering clouds, transformed into a jewel canopy, covering all the Buddhas here above. At that time, worlds of the ten directions penetrated without hindrance, like a single Buddha land.
Then the Buddha addressed Viśiṣṭacāritra and the assembly of Bodhisattvas: "The spiritual powers of the Buddhas are immeasurable, boundless, and inconceivable like this. If I, through these spiritual powers, throughout immeasurable, boundless hundreds of thousands of millions of incalculable kalpas, for the sake of entrustment, were to speak of the merits of this scripture, I could not exhaust them.
In essence: all dharmas possessed by the Tathāgata, all sovereign spiritual powers of the Tathāgata, all secret essential treasuries of the Tathāgata, and all profound matters of the Tathāgata are proclaimed and revealed in this scripture.
Therefore, you all, after the Tathāgata's extinction, should single-mindedly receive, uphold, read, recite, explain, copy, and practice it as taught. In whatever land, if there are those who receive, uphold, read, recite, explain, copy, or practice it as taught, or wherever copies of the scripture reside—whether in a garden, in a forest, beneath a tree, in a monastery, in a white-robed layman's house, in a palace hall, or in a mountain valley or wilderness—in all these places one should erect a stūpa and make offerings.
For what reason? You should know that this place is the place of awakening: here the Buddhas attained anuttarasamyaksambodhi, here the Buddhas turned the wheel of Dharma, and here the Buddhas entered parinirvāṇa."
Thereupon, the World-Honored One, wishing to restate this meaning, spoke gāthās:
The Buddhas, saviors of the world,
Abiding in great spiritual powers,
To delight wandering beings,
Manifest immeasurable spiritual powers;
Their tongues reach the Brahmā Heaven,
Their bodies emit countless lights;
For those who seek the Buddha path,
They manifest these rare matters.
The sound of the Buddhas clearing their throats,
And the sound of snapping their fingers,
Universally heard in lands of the ten directions,
The earth shaking in six ways.
Because after the Buddha's extinction,
One can uphold this scripture,
All the Buddhas rejoice,
Manifesting immeasurable spiritual powers.
To entrust this scripture,
Praising those who receive and uphold it,
Throughout immeasurable kalpas,
Still it cannot be exhausted.
The merits of this person,
Are boundless and without end,
Like open space in the ten directions,
Whose limits cannot be found.
Those who can uphold this scripture,
Have already seen me,
And have seen Prabhūtaratna Buddha,
And all the emanation Buddhas;
They also see me today,
Teaching and transforming Bodhisattvas.
Those who can uphold this scripture,
Cause me and my emanation bodies,
And the extinct Prabhūtaratna Buddha,
To completely rejoice.
Present Buddhas of the ten directions,
As well as past and future Buddhas,
They also behold and make offerings to,
And cause to attain joy.
What the Buddhas at the place of awakening
Attained as the secret essential Dharma,
Those who can uphold this scripture,
Before long will also attain.
Those who can uphold this scripture,
Regarding the meanings of all dharmas,
Their names and expressions,
Delight in speaking without end,
Like wind in open space,
Completely free of hindrances.
After the Tathāgata's extinction,
Knowing the scriptures spoken by the Buddha,
Their causal conditions and sequences,
Expounding according to the meaning as it is;
Like the light of the sun and moon,
Able to dispel all darkness.
This person traveling through the world,
Can extinguish the darkness of wandering beings,
Teaching immeasurable Bodhisattvas,
Ultimately to abide in the One Vehicle.
Therefore those who possess wisdom,
Hearing this benefit of merit,
After my extinction,
Should receive and uphold this scripture;
This person regarding the Buddha path,
Definitive and without doubt!
