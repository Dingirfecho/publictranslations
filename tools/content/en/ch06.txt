At that time, the World-Honored One, having spoken these gāthās, addressed the great assembly, proclaiming these words: "This disciple of mine, Mahākāśyapa, in a future age will attend upon and behold three million billion Buddhas, World-Honored Ones, making offerings, revering, honoring, and praising them, widely proclaiming the immeasurable great Dharma of the Buddhas.
In his final body, he will become a Buddha named Avabhāsaprabha Tathāgata, Arhat, Samyaksambuddha, Vidyācaraṇasampanna, Sugata, Lokavid, Anuttara, Puruṣadamyasārathi, Śāstā Devamanuṣyāṇām, Buddha, Bhagavān. His land will be named Prabhāsa-kalyāṇa, and his kalpa will be named Mahāvyūha.
The Buddha's lifespan will be twelve minor kalpas; the Correct Dharma will abide in the world for twenty minor kalpas, and the Semblance Dharma will also abide for twenty minor kalpas. His realm will be adorned, free of all filth, evil, shards, rubble, thorns, briars, feces, and urine. That land will be flat and level, without elevations, depressions, pits, ditches, or mounds. Lapis lazuli will form the ground, with rows of jewel trees; cords of gold will border the roads, strewn with jewel flowers, completely pure all around.
The Bodhisattvas of that land will be immeasurable thousands of billions, and the assemblies of Śrāvakas will also be countless. There will be no activities of Mara; although there will be Mara and the subjects of Mara, they will all protect the Buddha-Dharma."
Thereupon, the World-Honored One, wishing to restate this meaning, spoke gāthās:
I tell the bhikṣus:
"With the Buddha eye,
I see that this Kāśyapa,
In a future age,
After countless kalpas have passed,
Will become a Buddha.
In future lives,
He will make offerings to and attend upon
Three million billion
Buddhas, World-Honored Ones.
For the sake of the Buddha's wisdom,
He will purely cultivate Brahma conduct.
Having made offerings to the supreme
Honored Among Two-Legged Beings,
He will cultivate
All unexcelled wisdom.
In his final body,
He will become a Buddha.
His land will be pure,
With lapis lazuli as its ground;
Many jewel trees
Lining the sides of the roads,
Gold cords bordering the paths,
Delighting those who see them.
Constantly emitting fine fragrance,
Scattering various renowned flowers,
Various rare and wondrous things
Serving as adornments.
That ground will be level and flat,
Without hills or pits.
The assemblies of Bodhisattvas
Will be beyond calculation;
Their minds gentle and subdued,
Attaining great spiritual superknowledges,
Upholding the Buddhas'
Great Vehicle scriptures.
The assemblies of Śrāvakas,
Uncontaminated in their final bodies,
Children of the Dharma King,
Will also be beyond calculation,
So that even with the divine eye,
None could count or know them.
That Buddha's lifespan
Will be twelve minor kalpas;
The Correct Dharma will abide in the world
For twenty minor kalpas;
The Semblance Dharma will also abide
For twenty minor kalpas.
The World-Honored One Avabhāsaprabha,
His activities will be like this."
At that time, Mahāmaudgalyāyana, Subhūti, and Mahākātyāyana were all awestruck. Single-mindedly joining their palms, they gazed up at the venerable countenance without glancing away for an instant, and together with one voice spoke gāthās:
Great Heroic, Mighty World-Honored One,
Dharma King of the Śākyas,
Out of compassion for us,
Pray bestow the Buddha's voice.
If you know our deep hearts
And deign to bestow prophecies upon us,
It will be like sprinkling amṛta,
Removing heat and attaining coolness.
Like one coming from a famine-stricken land,
Suddenly encountering a great king's feast,
Yet harboring doubt and fear in heart,
Not daring to eat immediately;
If receiving the king's command,
Only then daring to eat.
We too are like this;
Constantly reflecting on the faults of the Small Vehicle,
We know not how
To attain the Buddha's unexcelled wisdom.
Although hearing the Buddha's voice
Stating that we will become Buddhas,
Our hearts still harbor worry and fear,
Like not yet daring to eat;
If receiving the Buddha's prophecy,
Only then will we be happily at peace.
Great Heroic, Mighty World-Honored One,
Constantly wishing to bring peace to the world,
Pray bestow prophecies upon us,
As the hungry require permission to eat.
Then the World-Honored One, knowing the thoughts in the minds of his great disciples, addressed the bhikṣus: "This Subhūti, in a future age, will attend upon and behold three million billion inconceivable Buddhas, making offerings, revering, honoring, and praising them, constantly cultivating Brahma conduct, and fulfilling the Bodhisattva path.
In his final body, he will become a Buddha named Śaśiketu Tathāgata, Arhat, Samyaksambuddha, Vidyācaraṇasampanna, Sugata, Lokavid, Anuttara, Puruṣadamyasārathi, Śāstā Devamanuṣyāṇām, Buddha, Bhagavān. His kalpa will be named Ratnaprabhāsa, and his land will be named Ratnasaṃbhava. That land will be level and flat, with crystal as its ground, adorned with jewel trees, free of hills, pits, sand, rubble, thorns, briars, feces, and urine; jewel flowers will cover the ground, completely pure all around. The people of that land will all dwell on jewel platforms and exquisite towers.
The Śrāvaka disciples will be immeasurable and boundless, beyond calculation or parables to know. The assemblies of Bodhisattvas will be countless thousands of millions of billions of inconceivable numbers. The Buddha's lifespan will be twelve minor kalpas; the Correct Dharma will abide in the world for twenty minor kalpas, and the Semblance Dharma will also abide for twenty minor kalpas. That Buddha will constantly dwell in open space expounding the Dharma for the multitude, liberating immeasurable Bodhisattvas and Śrāvaka assemblies."
Thereupon, the World-Honored One, wishing to restate this meaning, spoke gāthās:
Assemblies of bhikṣus,
I now address you all;
You should all single-mindedly
Listen to what I speak:
My great disciple,
Subhūti,
Will become a Buddha,
Named Śaśiketu.
He will make offerings to countless
Tens of billions of Buddhas;
Following the practices of the Buddhas,
Gradually fulfilling the Great Path.
In his final body, he will attain
The thirty-two marks,
Upright, handsome, and subtle,
Just like a jewel mountain.
That Buddha land
Will be foremost in purity and adornment;
Wandering beings who behold it
Will all love and delight in it;
The Buddha within it
Will liberate immeasurable multitudes.
Within that Buddha's Dharma,
There will be many Bodhisattvas,
All of sharp faculties,
Turning the irreversible wheel;
That land will constantly
Be adorned with Bodhisattvas.
The assemblies of Śrāvakas
Will be beyond reckoning,
All attaining the three knowledges,
Endowed with the six spiritual superknowledges,
Dwelling in the eight liberations,
Possessing great majesty and virtue.
When that Buddha expounds the Dharma,
He will display immeasurable
Spiritual transformations,
Inconceivable.
Devas and humans,
Their numbers like Ganges sands,
Will all join palms together,
Listening to and receiving the Buddha's words.
That Buddha's lifespan
Will be twelve minor kalpas;
The Correct Dharma will abide in the world
For twenty minor kalpas;
The Semblance Dharma will also abide
For twenty minor kalpas.
Then the World-Honored One further addressed the assembly of bhikṣus: "I now tell you: this Mahākātyāyana in a future age, with various articles of offering, will make offerings to and serve eight hundred billion Buddhas, revering and honoring them. After the extinction of those Buddhas, he will erect stūpas and shrines for each, a thousand yojanas in height, and five hundred yojanas in length and breadth, made of the seven treasures: gold, silver, lapis lazuli, mother-of-pearl, agate, pearls, and carnelian; with flowers, necklaces, paste incenses, powdered incenses, burning incenses, silk canopies, banners, and streamers, he will make offerings to the stūpas and shrines. After this has passed, he will further make offerings to twenty thousand billion Buddhas in the same manner.
Having made offerings to these Buddhas, fulfilling the Bodhisattva path, he will become a Buddha named Jāmbūnadakāñcanaprabhāsa Tathāgata, Arhat, Samyaksambuddha, Vidyācaraṇasampanna, Sugata, Lokavid, Anuttara, Puruṣadamyasārathi, Śāstā Devamanuṣyāṇām, Buddha, Bhagavān. His land will be level and flat, with crystal as its ground, adorned with jewel trees; cords of gold will border the roads, wondrous flowers will cover the ground, completely pure all around, delighting those who see it. It will be free from the four evil destinies: hell, hungry ghost, animal, and asura destinies. There will be many devas, humans, Śrāvaka assemblies, and Bodhisattvas, immeasurable tens of billions adorning that land. The Buddha's lifespan will be twelve minor kalpas; the Correct Dharma will abide in the world for twenty minor kalpas, and the Semblance Dharma will also abide for twenty minor kalpas."
Thereupon, the World-Honored One, wishing to restate this meaning, spoke gāthās:
Assemblies of bhikṣus,
All listen with one mind;
As I have spoken,
It is genuine, real, and without difference.
This Kātyāyana,
With various
Wonderful articles of offering,
Will make offerings to the Buddhas.
After the extinction of the Buddhas,
Erecting seven-jeweled stūpas,
And with flowers and incense,
Making offerings to the relics.
In his final body,
Attaining the Buddha's wisdom,
He will accomplish equal and right awakening.
His land will be pure,
Liberating immeasurable
Tens of billions of wandering beings,
Universally by the ten directions
Receiving offerings.
The light of the Buddha
None will be able to surpass.
That Buddha will be named
Jāmbūnadakāñcana.
Bodhisattvas and Śrāvakas,
Severing all existence,
Immeasurable and countless,
Will adorn his land.
Then the World-Honored One further addressed the great assembly: "I now tell you: this Mahāmaudgalyāyana will, with various articles of offering, make offerings to eight thousand Buddhas, revering and honoring them. After the extinction of those Buddhas, he will erect stūpas and shrines for each, a thousand yojanas in height, and five hundred yojanas in length and breadth, made of the seven treasures: gold, silver, lapis lazuli, mother-of-pearl, agate, pearls, and carnelian; with flowers, necklaces, paste incenses, powdered incenses, burning incenses, silk canopies, banners, and streamers, he will make offerings. After this has passed, he will further make offerings to two million billion Buddhas in the same manner.
He will become a Buddha named Tamālapattracandana-gandha Tathāgata, Arhat, Samyaksambuddha, Vidyācaraṇasampanna, Sugata, Lokavid, Anuttara, Puruṣadamyasārathi, Śāstā Devamanuṣyāṇām, Buddha, Bhagavān. His kalpa will be named Ratipūrṇa, and his land will be named Manobhirāma. That land will be level and flat, with crystal as its ground, adorned with jewel trees, scattered with pearl flowers, completely pure all around, delighting those who see it. There will be many devas, humans, Bodhisattvas, and Śrāvakas, their numbers immeasurable. The Buddha's lifespan will be twenty-four minor kalpas; the Correct Dharma will abide in the world for forty minor kalpas, and the Semblance Dharma will also abide for forty minor kalpas."
Thereupon, the World-Honored One, wishing to restate this meaning, spoke gāthās:
This disciple of mine,
Mahāmaudgalyāyana,
Having cast off this body,
Will behold eight thousand
And two million billion
Buddhas, World-Honored Ones.
For the sake of the Buddha path,
Making offerings and revering them,
In the presence of the Buddhas,
Constantly cultivating Brahma conduct,
Throughout immeasurable kalpas,
Upholding the Buddha-Dharma.
After the Buddhas' extinction,
Erecting seven-jeweled stūpas,
With lofty golden spires,
Flowers, incense, and music,
Making offerings
To the stūpas and shrines of the Buddhas.
Gradually fulfilling
The Bodhisattva path,
In the land of Manobhirāma,
He will become a Buddha.
Named Tamālapatra
Candana-gandha.
That Buddha's lifespan
Will be twenty-four kalpas;
Constantly for devas and humans,
He will expound the Buddha path.
Śrāvakas will be immeasurable,
Like the sands of the Ganges,
With the three knowledges and six superknowledges,
Possessing great majesty and virtue.
Bodhisattvas will be countless,
Firm in resolve and vigorous,
Regarding the Buddha's wisdom,
All irreversible.
After the Buddha's extinction,
The Correct Dharma will abide
For forty minor kalpas;
The Semblance Dharma will be likewise.
All my disciples,
Endowed with majesty and virtue,
Five hundred in number,
Will all receive prophecies.
In future ages,
All will become Buddhas.
Regarding the past causal conditions
Between me and you all,
I will now speak of them;
Listen well, all of you!"
