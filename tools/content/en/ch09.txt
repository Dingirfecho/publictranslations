On Practitioners in Training and Beyond Training
At that time, Ānanda and Rāhula had this thought: "We constantly reflect: 'If we could receive a prophecy, would that not be joyful!'" Immediately rising from their seats, they went before the Buddha, bowed with heads touching his feet, and addressed the Buddha together:
"World-Honored One! We too should have a share in this; only the Tathāgata is our refuge. Furthermore, we are known to all devas, humans, and asuras in the world: Ānanda is constantly the attendant, guarding the Dharma treasury; Rāhula is the son of the Buddha. If the Buddha confers upon us the prophecy of anuttarasamyaksambodhi, our vows will be fulfilled, and the hopes of the assembly will also be satisfied."
At that time, two thousand Śrāvaka disciples in training and beyond training rose from their seats, bared their right shoulders, went before the Buddha, single-mindedly joined their palms, gazed upon the World-Honored One, and, sharing the wish of Ānanda and Rāhula, stood on one side.
Then the Buddha addressed Ānanda: "You in a future age will become a Buddha named Sāgaravaradharabuddhivikrīḍitābhijña Tathāgata, Arhat, Samyaksambuddha, Vidyācaraṇasampanna, Sugata, Lokavid, Anuttara, Puruṣadamyasārathi, Śāstā Devamanuṣyāṇām, Buddha, Bhagavān.
You will make offerings to sixty-two billion Buddhas, protecting their Dharma treasuries, and afterward will attain anuttarasamyaksambodhi. You will teach and transform Bodhisattvas equal to the sands of twenty thousand billion Ganges rivers, causing them to accomplish anuttarasamyaksambodhi.
Your land will be named Anavanāmitavaijayantī, pure, with lapis lazuli as its ground. The kalpa will be named Manojñasvarasaṃpūrṇa. That Buddha's lifespan will be immeasurable thousands of billions of incalculable kalpas; if someone were to calculate it through thousands of billions of immeasurable incalculable kalpas, they could not know it. The Correct Dharma will abide in the world for double his lifespan, and the Semblance Dharma will abide for double the Correct Dharma again.
Ānanda! This Sāgaravaradharabuddhivikrīḍitābhijña Buddha will be praised by immeasurable thousands of billions of Ganges sands of Buddhas, Tathāgatas of the ten directions, extolling his virtues."
Thereupon, the World-Honored One, wishing to restate this meaning, spoke gāthās:
I now speak amidst the Saṅgha:
Ānanda, the upholder of Dharma,
Will make offerings to the Buddhas,
And afterward accomplish right awakening.
Named Sāgaravaradharabuddhi-
Vikrīḍitābhijña Buddha.
His land will be pure,
Named Anavanāmitavaijayantī;
Teaching Bodhisattvas,
Their numbers like Ganges sands.
The Buddha will possess great majesty and virtue,
His fame filling the ten directions.
His lifespan will have no measure,
Because of pitying wandering beings;
The Correct Dharma will be double his lifespan,
The Semblance Dharma double that again.
Countless wandering beings,
Like the sands of the Ganges,
Within this Buddha's Dharma,
Will plant causal conditions for the Buddha path.
At that time, eight thousand newly resolved Bodhisattvas in the assembly had this thought: "We have not yet heard great Bodhisattvas receive such prophecies; for what causal condition do these Śrāvakas receive such resolutions?"
Then the World-Honored One, knowing the thoughts in the minds of the Bodhisattvas, addressed them: "Good children! Ānanda and I, under Dharmagahanābhyudgatarāja Buddha, simultaneously generated the mind of anuttarasamyaksambodhi. Ānanda constantly delighted in learning, while I constantly exerted diligence and vigor. Therefore, I have already accomplished anuttarasamyaksambodhi, while Ānanda protects my Dharma, and will also protect the Dharma treasuries of future Buddhas, teaching and accomplishing assemblies of Bodhisattvas. His original vow was like this, and therefore he receives this prophecy."
Ānanda, personally before the Buddha, hearing his prophecy and the adornments of his land, his vows fulfilled, rejoiced greatly, attaining what he had never had before. Immediately he recalled the Dharma treasuries of immeasurable thousands of billions of past Buddhas, penetrating them without hindrance, just as what he had heard today, and also recognized his original vow.
Thereupon, Ānanda spoke gāthās:
The World-Honored One is exceedingly rare,
Causing me to recall the past,
The Dharma of immeasurable Buddhas,
Just as what I hear today.
I now have no further doubts,
Dwelling peacefully in the Buddha path;
Through expedient means serving as an attendant,
Protecting the Dharma of the Buddhas.
Then the Buddha addressed Rāhula: "You in a future age will become a Buddha named Saptaratnapadmavikrāmin Tathāgata, Arhat, Samyaksambuddha, Vidyācaraṇasampanna, Sugata, Lokavid, Anuttara, Puruṣadamyasārathi, Śāstā Devamanuṣyāṇām, Buddha, Bhagavān.
You will make offerings to Buddhas, Tathāgatas equal to the dust motes of ten worlds, constantly serving as the eldest son of those Buddhas, just as you do now. The adornments of that Saptaratnapadmavikrāmin Buddha's land, the length of his lifespan and kalpas, the disciples he transforms, and the Correct and Semblance Dharmas will be no different from those of Sāgaravaradharabuddhivikrīḍitābhijña Tathāgata; you will also serve as this Buddha's eldest son. After this has passed, you will attain anuttarasamyaksambodhi."
Thereupon, the World-Honored One, wishing to restate this meaning, spoke gāthās:
When I was a prince,
Rāhula was my eldest son;
Now that I have accomplished the Buddha path,
He receives the Dharma as a Dharma-child.
In future ages,
He will behold immeasurable billions of Buddhas,
Serving as the eldest son to them all,
Single-mindedly seeking the Buddha path.
Rāhula's hidden practice,
Only I can know;
He manifests as my eldest son,
To reveal it to wandering beings.
Immeasurable billions of thousands,
His merits cannot be counted;
Dwelling peacefully in the Buddha-Dharma,
To seek the unexcelled path.
At that time, the World-Honored One beheld the two thousand practitioners in training and beyond training, their minds gentle, tranquil, and pure, single-mindedly gazing upon the Buddha. The Buddha addressed Ānanda: "Do you see these two thousand practitioners in training and beyond training?"
"Yes, I have seen them."
"Ānanda! These people will make offerings to Buddhas, Tathāgatas equal to the dust motes of fifty worlds, revering and honoring them, protecting their Dharma treasuries. At the end, simultaneously in lands of the ten directions, each will become a Buddha, all with the same name, Ratnaketurāja Tathāgata, Arhat, Samyaksambuddha, Vidyācaraṇasampanna, Sugata, Lokavid, Anuttara, Puruṣadamyasārathi, Śāstā Devamanuṣyāṇām, Buddha, Bhagavān. Their lifespan will be one kalpa. The adornments of their lands, their Śrāvakas, Bodhisattvas, Correct Dharma, and Semblance Dharma will all be equal."
Thereupon, the World-Honored One, wishing to restate this meaning, spoke gāthās:
These two thousand Śrāvakas,
Now standing before me,
I bestow prophecies upon them all;
In the future they will become Buddhas.
The Buddhas they will make offerings to,
Are like the dust motes spoken above;
Protecting their Dharma treasuries,
Afterward they will accomplish right awakening.
Each in lands of the ten directions,
All with the same name,
Simultaneously sitting at places of awakening,
To realize unexcelled wisdom.
All named Ratnaketurāja.
Their lands and disciples,
Correct Dharma and Semblance Dharma,
Will all be equal without difference.
All through spiritual powers,
Liberating wandering beings of the ten directions;
Their fame universally pervading,
Gradually entering into nirvāṇa.
At that time, the two thousand practitioners in training and beyond training, hearing the Buddha's prophecy, rejoiced and leaped, speaking gāthās:
The World-Honored One's bright lamp of wisdom!
Hearing the sound of bestowed prophecy,
Our hearts are filled with joy,
As if sprinkled with amṛta.
