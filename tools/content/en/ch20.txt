At that time, the Buddha addressed Mahāsthāmaprāpta Bodhisattva Mahāsattva: "You should now know! If bhikṣus, bhikṣuṇīs, upāsakas, or upāsikās who uphold the Lotus Sūtra are reviled, abused, or slandered by anyone with an evil mouth, they incur great sinful retribution, as spoken before; the merits attained by the upholders are as spoken just now: the purity of eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, and mind.
Mahāsthāmaprāpta! In the distant past, immeasurable, boundless, inconceivable incalculable kalpas ago, there was a Buddha named Bhīṣmagarjitasvararāja Tathāgata, Arhat, Samyaksambuddha, Vidyācaraṇasampanna, Sugata, Lokavid, Anuttara, Puruṣadamyasārathi, Śāstā Devamanuṣyāṇām, Buddha, Bhagavān. His kalpa was named Vinirbhoga, and his land was named Mahāsaṃbhava.
That Bhīṣmagarjitasvararāja Buddha, in that world, expounded the Dharma for devas, humans, and asuras: for those seeking to be Śrāvakas, expounding the Dharma responding to the Four Truths, to cross beyond birth, old age, sickness, and death, to ultimate nirvāṇa; for those seeking to be Pratyekabuddhas, expounding the Dharma responding to the Twelve Links of Dependent Origination; for Bodhisattvas, on account of anuttarasamyaksambodhi, expounding the Dharma responding to the Six Pāramitās, to ultimate Buddha wisdom.
Mahāsthāmaprāpta! The lifespan of this Bhīṣmagarjitasvararāja Buddha was forty thousand billion inconceivable numbers of Ganges sands of kalpas; the kalpas of the Correct Dharma abiding in the world were equal to the dust motes of a single Jambudvīpa; the kalpas of the Semblance Dharma abiding were equal to the dust motes of the four continents. That Buddha, having benefited wandering beings, passed into extinction.
After the Correct Dharma and Semblance Dharma were entirely exhausted, in that land another Buddha appeared, also named Bhīṣmagarjitasvararāja Tathāgata, Arhat, Samyaksambuddha, Vidyācaraṇasampanna, Sugata, Lokavid, Anuttara, Puruṣadamyasārathi, Śāstā Devamanuṣyāṇām, Buddha, Bhagavān. In this manner successively, there were twenty thousand billion Buddhas, all with the same name.
After the initial Bhīṣmagarjitasvararāja Tathāgata passed into extinction, and after the Correct Dharma ended, during the Semblance Dharma, bhikṣus of arrogant pride possessed great power and influence. At that time, there was a Bodhisattva bhikṣu named Sadāparibhūta.
Mahāsthāmaprāpta! For what causal condition was he named Sadāparibhūta? Whenever this bhikṣu saw anyone—whether bhikṣu, bhikṣuṇī, upāsaka, or upāsikā—he bowed down to them in homage, praised them, and spoke these words: 'I deeply revere you all; I dare not slight or despise you. For what reason? You all practice the Bodhisattva path and will become Buddhas.'
And this bhikṣu did not specifically read or recite the scriptures, but merely practiced bowing in homage; even seeing the fourfold assembly from afar, he would intentionally go to bow in homage, praise them, and speak these words: 'I dare not slight you; you will all become Buddhas.'
Among the fourfold assembly, there were those who gave rise to anger and impure minds, reviling him with evil mouths, saying: 'Where does this ignorant bhikṣu come from? Claiming of himself: "I do not slight you," and conferring prophecies upon us that we will become Buddhas! We have no use for such false prophecies.'
Passing through many years in this manner, constantly reviled, he gave rise to no anger, constantly speaking: 'You will become Buddhas.' When he spoke these words, people sometimes struck him with sticks, wood, tiles, or stones; fleeing and standing far away, he still proclaimed in a loud voice: 'I dare not slight you; you will all become Buddhas!'
Because he constantly spoke these words, the bhikṣus, bhikṣuṇīs, upāsakas, and upāsikās of arrogant pride named him Sadāparibhūta.
When this bhikṣu approached death, in open space he heard the twenty thousand billion verses of the Lotus Sūtra previously expounded by Bhīṣmagarjitasvararāja Buddha, fully able to receive and uphold them all, immediately attaining the purity of the eye faculty, and the purity of ear, nose, tongue, body, and mind faculties as spoken above. Having attained the purity of these six faculties, his lifespan was lengthened by two hundred million billion inconceivable years, and he expounded this Lotus Sūtra widely for people.
At that time, the fourfold assembly of arrogant pride—bhikṣus, bhikṣuṇīs, upāsakas, and upāsikās—who had despised this person and given him the name Sadāparibhūta, seeing that he had attained great spiritual powers, the power of joyful and unhindered eloquence, and the power of great wholesome tranquility, hearing what he expounded, all believed, submitted, and followed him.
This Bodhisattva further transformed thousands of billions of multitudes, causing them to abide in anuttarasamyaksambodhi. After his life ended, he encountered two thousand billion Buddhas, all named Candrasūryapradīpa; within their Dharma, he expounded this Lotus Sūtra.
By this causal condition, he further encountered two thousand billion Buddhas, all named Meghasvaradīparāja; within the Dharma of these Buddhas, receiving, upholding, reading, reciting, and expounding this scripture for the fourfold assembly, he attained this purity of the ordinary eye, and the purity of ear, nose, tongue, body, and mind faculties, expounding the Dharma amidst the fourfold assembly with mind free of fear.
Mahāsthāmaprāpta! This Bodhisattva Mahāsattva Sadāparibhūta made offerings to such numerous Buddhas as these, revering, honoring, praising them, and planting roots of virtue; afterward, he further encountered thousands of billions of Buddhas, also expounding this scripture within the Dharma of those Buddhas, his merits accomplished, destined to become a Buddha.
Mahāsthāmaprāpta! What do you think: could the Bodhisattva Sadāparibhūta at that time have been someone else? It was I myself. If in past lives I had not received, upheld, read, recited, and expounded this scripture for others, I would not have been able swiftly to attain anuttarasamyaksambodhi. Because under past Buddhas I received, upheld, read, recited, and expounded this scripture for others, I swiftly attained anuttarasamyaksambodhi.
Mahāsthāmaprāpta! At that time, the fourfold assembly—bhikṣus, bhikṣuṇīs, upāsakas, and upāsikās—because with angry minds they despised and scorned me, for two hundred billion kalpas never encountered a Buddha, never heard the Dharma, and never saw the Saṅgha; for a thousand kalpas in the Avīci Hell they underwent great suffering and affliction. Having exhausted this transgression, they again encountered Bodhisattva Sadāparibhūta, who taught and transformed them in anuttarasamyaksambodhi.
Mahāsthāmaprāpta, what do you think: could those in the fourfold assembly at that time who constantly despised this Bodhisattva have been someone else? They are the five hundred Bodhisattvas in this assembly, such as Bhadrapāla; the five hundred bhikṣus, such as Siṃhacandra; and the five hundred upāsakas, such as Sugatacetanā, who are all irreversible from anuttarasamyaksambodhi!
Mahāsthāmaprāpta! You should know that this Lotus Sūtra greatly benefits all Bodhisattva Mahāsattvas, able to cause them to arrive at anuttarasamyaksambodhi. Therefore, Bodhisattva Mahāsattvas, after the Tathāgata's extinction, should constantly receive, uphold, read, recite, explain, and copy this scripture."
Thereupon, the World-Honored One, wishing to restate this meaning, spoke gāthās:
In the past there was a Buddha,
Named Bhīṣmagarjitasvararāja,
Of immeasurable spiritual wisdom,
Guiding all beings;
Devas, humans, Nagas, and spirits,
Together made offerings to him.
After this Buddha passed into extinction,
When the Dharma was about to end,
There was a Bodhisattva,
Named Sadāparibhūta.
At that time the fourfold assembly,
Were attached to dharmas.
Bodhisattva Paribhūta,
Went to where they were,
And addressed them:
"I do not slight you;
You practice the path,
And will all become Buddhas."
All the people, hearing this,
Slighted, defamed, and reviled him;
Bodhisattva Paribhūta,
Was able to endure it.
When his transgressions were exhausted,
Approaching the end of his life,
He attained hearing this scripture,
And his six faculties were purified.
Through spiritual powers,
His lifespan was lengthened,
And again for all people,
He widely expounded this scripture.
The multitudes attached to dharmas,
All received the Bodhisattva's
Teaching, transformation, and accomplishment,
Causing them to abide in the Buddha path.
When Paribhūta's life ended,
He encountered countless Buddhas;
Because of expounding this scripture,
He attained immeasurable merit;
Gradually fulfilling virtues,
He swiftly accomplished the Buddha path.
The Paribhūta of that time,
Was none other than myself.
The fourfold assembly at that time,
Who were attached to dharmas,
Hearing Paribhūta say:
"You will become Buddhas";
By this causal condition,
Encountered countless Buddhas.
The assembly of five hundred Bodhisattvas
In this gathering,
Along with the fourfold assembly,
Pure faithful men and women,
Now standing before me,
Listening to the Dharma, are them.
I in past lives,
Urged these people,
To hear and receive this scripture,
The foremost Dharma;
Opening and teaching people,
Causing them to abide in nirvāṇa;
Life after life receiving and upholding
Such a scripture as this.
Throughout tens of billions of kalpas,
Down to the inconceivable,
Only then at certain times does one hear
This Lotus Sūtra.
Throughout tens of billions of kalpas,
Down to the inconceivable,
The Buddhas, World-Honored Ones,
Only at certain times expound this scripture.
Therefore practitioners,
After the Buddha's extinction,
Hearing such a scripture as this,
Should not give rise to doubt;
They should single-mindedly
Widely expound this scripture,
Life after life encountering Buddhas,
Swiftly accomplishing the Buddha path.
