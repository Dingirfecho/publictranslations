At that time, Akṣayamati Bodhisattva arose from his seat, bared his right shoulder, joined his palms toward the Buddha, and spoke these words: "World-Honored One! By what causal condition is Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara named Avalokiteśvara?"
The Buddha addressed Akṣayamati Bodhisattva: "Good son! If there are immeasurable hundreds of thousands of millions of wandering beings undergoing various sufferings and afflictions, who, hearing of this Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara, single-mindedly recite his name, Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara will immediately perceive their sounds, and they will all attain liberation.
If someone upholds the name of this Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara, even if entering a great fire, the fire cannot burn them, through the majestic spiritual power of this Bodhisattva.
If swept away by great waters, reciting his name, they will immediately reach a shallow place.
If there are hundreds of thousands of millions of wandering beings who, seeking gold, silver, lapis lazuli, mother-of-pearl, agate, coral, amber, pearls, and other treasures, enter the great ocean; and suppose a black wind blows their ships, drifting into the land of rākṣasa ghosts: if among them there is even a single person who recites the name of Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara, all those people will attain liberation from the perils of rākṣasas.
By this causal condition, he is named Avalokiteśvara.
If someone about to be harmed recites the name of Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara, the knives and sticks held by the assailant will instantly break into pieces, and they will attain liberation.
If a great three-thousand-fold worlds were filled with yakṣas and rākṣasas wishing to torment people, hearing them recite the name of Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara, those evil ghosts could not even look upon them with an evil eye, how much less inflict harm!
Even if someone, whether guilty or innocent, has their body bound in fetters, stocks, cangues, and chains, reciting the name of Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara, all will break apart, and they will immediately attain liberation.
If a great three-thousand-fold worlds were filled with dangerous bandits, and a merchant leader leading various traders carrying valuable treasures travels along a perilous road; among them, one person speaks out: 'Good children! Do not harbor fear! You should all single-mindedly recite the name of Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara. This Bodhisattva can bestow fearlessness upon wandering beings. If you recite his name, from these dangerous bandits you will attain liberation!' All the traders, hearing this, simultaneously raise their voices: 'Homage to Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara!' Because of reciting his name, they immediately attain liberation.
Akṣayamati! The majestic spiritual power of Bodhisattva Mahāsattva Avalokiteśvara is towering like this!
If wandering beings have excessive desire, constantly remembering and revering Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara, they will abandon desire.
If having excessive anger, constantly remembering and revering Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara, they will abandon anger.
If having excessive ignorance, constantly remembering and revering Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara, they will abandon ignorance.
Akṣayamati! Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara possesses such great majestic spiritual powers, extensively bringing benefit. Therefore, wandering beings should constantly be mindful of him in their hearts.
If a woman wishes to seek a son, bowing down and making offerings to Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara, she will give birth to a son of merit, virtue, and wisdom; if she wishes to seek a daughter, she will give birth to an upright, handsome daughter, who has planted roots of virtue in past lives, loved and revered by all.
Akṣayamati! Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara possesses such power. If wandering beings revere and bow down to Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara, their blessings will not be in vain. Therefore, wandering beings should all receive and uphold the name of Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara.
Akṣayamati! If someone were to receive and uphold the names of Bodhisattvas equal to the sands of sixty-two billion Ganges rivers, and for the rest of their life make offerings of food, drink, clothing, bedding, and medicine: what do you think: is the merit of this good man or good woman plentiful or not?"
Akṣayamati said: "Extremely plentiful, World-Honored One!"
The Buddha said: "If someone receives and upholds the name of Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara, and even for an instant bows down and makes offerings: the blessings of these two persons are equal without difference, and throughout hundreds of thousands of millions of kalpas cannot be exhausted.
Akṣayamati! Receiving and upholding the name of Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara attains such immeasurable, boundless benefits of merit."
Akṣayamati Bodhisattva addressed the Buddha: "World-Honored One! How does Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara travel in this Sahā world? How does he expound the Dharma for wandering beings? How does he exercise the power of expedient means?"
The Buddha addressed Akṣayamati Bodhisattva: "Good son! If wandering beings in a land should be liberated through the body of a Buddha, Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara immediately manifests the body of a Buddha to expound the Dharma;
If they should be liberated through the body of a Pratyekabuddha, he immediately manifests the body of a Pratyekabuddha to expound the Dharma;
If they should be liberated through the body of a Śrāvaka, he immediately manifests the body of a Śrāvaka to expound the Dharma;
If they should be liberated through the body of a Brahmā King, he immediately manifests the body of a Brahmā King to expound the Dharma;
If they should be liberated through the body of Śakra, he immediately manifests the body of Śakra to expound the Dharma;
If they should be liberated through the body of Īśvara, he immediately manifests the body of Īśvara to expound the Dharma;
If they should be liberated through the body of Maheśvara, he immediately manifests the body of Maheśvara to expound the Dharma;
If they should be liberated through the body of a heavenly great general, he immediately manifests the body of a heavenly great general to expound the Dharma;
If they should be liberated through the body of Vaiśravaṇa, he immediately manifests the body of Vaiśravaṇa to expound the Dharma;
If they should be liberated through the body of a petty king, he immediately manifests the body of a petty king to expound the Dharma;
If they should be liberated through the body of an elder, he immediately manifests the body of an elder to expound the Dharma;
If they should be liberated through the body of a householder, he immediately manifests the body of a householder to expound the Dharma;
If they should be liberated through the body of an official, he immediately manifests the body of an official to expound the Dharma;
If they should be liberated through the body of a brāhmaṇa, he immediately manifests the body of a brāhmaṇa to expound the Dharma;
If they should be liberated through the body of a bhikṣu, bhikṣuṇī, upāsaka, or upāsikā, he immediately manifests the body of a bhikṣu, bhikṣuṇī, upāsaka, or upāsikā to expound the Dharma;
If they should be liberated through the body of the wife of an elder, householder, official, or brāhmaṇa, he immediately manifests the body of a woman to expound the Dharma;
If they should be liberated through the body of a boy or girl, he immediately manifests the body of a boy or girl to expound the Dharma;
If they should be liberated through the bodies of devas, nāgas, yakṣas, gandharvas, asuras, garuḍas, kiṃnaras, mahoragas, humans, or non-humans, he immediately manifests them all to expound the Dharma;
If they should be liberated through the body of Vajrapāṇi, he immediately manifests the body of Vajrapāṇi to expound the Dharma.
Akṣayamati! This Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara has accomplished such merits, through various forms traveling in lands to liberate wandering beings. Therefore, you all should single-mindedly make offerings to Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara. This Bodhisattva Mahāsattva Avalokiteśvara can bestow fearlessness amidst terrors and pressing perils; therefore, in this Sahā world, all call him the Bestower of Fearlessness."
Akṣayamati Bodhisattva addressed the Buddha: "World-Honored One! I should now make an offering to Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara."
Immediately he unfastened from his neck a necklace of precious pearls worth hundreds of thousands of pieces of gold, gave it to him, and said: "Virtuous one! Accept this Dharma offering of precious pearl necklaces."
At that time, Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara was unwilling to accept it. Akṣayamati addressed Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara again: "Virtuous one! Out of compassion for us, accept this necklace."
Then the Buddha addressed Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara: "Out of compassion for this Akṣayamati Bodhisattva, the fourfold assembly, devas, Nagas, yakṣas, gandharvas, asuras, garuḍas, kiṃnaras, mahoragas, humans, and non-humans, accept this necklace."
Immediately Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara, out of compassion for the fourfold assembly, devas, Nagas, humans, and non-humans, accepted the necklace, divided it into two parts, presenting one part to Śākyamuni Buddha, and presenting one part to the stūpa of Prabhūtaratna Buddha.
"Akṣayamati! Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara possesses such sovereign spiritual powers, traveling in the Sahā world."
Thereupon, Akṣayamati Bodhisattva inquired in gāthās:
Endowed with wondrous marks, World-Honored One,
I now question you again:
By what causal condition,
Is this Buddha-child named Avalokiteśvara?
The Honored One endowed with wondrous marks,
Answered Akṣayamati in gāthās:
Listen to the conduct of Avalokiteśvara,
Skillfully responding in all directions;
His vast vows are deep as the ocean,
Inconceivable through kalpas;
Attending upon thousands of billions of Buddhas,
Generating great pure vows.
I will briefly speak for you:
Hearing his name and beholding his body,
Mindfulness in heart is not in vain,
Able to extinguish all sufferings of existence.
Even if someone harboring harmful intent,
Pushes one into a great fire pit,
Mindful of that power of Avalokiteśvara,
The fire pit transforms into a pool of water.
Or if drifting upon the great ocean,
Beset by perils of Nagas, fish, and ghosts,
Mindful of that power of Avalokiteśvara,
Waves cannot drown them.
Or if on the peak of Mount Sumeru,
One is pushed down by someone,
Mindful of that power of Avalokiteśvara,
Like the sun one abides in open space.
Or if pursued by evil people,
Falling from a Diamond Mountain,
Mindful of that power of Avalokiteśvara,
Not a single hair can be harmed.
Or if surrounded by hostile bandits,
Each holding knives to inflict harm,
Mindful of that power of Avalokiteśvara,
They immediately give rise to compassionate minds.
Or if encountering suffering under royal execution,
Approaching death at the place of punishment,
Mindful of that power of Avalokiteśvara,
The knives instantly break into pieces.
Or if imprisoned in stocks and chains,
Hands and feet bound in fetters,
Mindful of that power of Avalokiteśvara,
They are broken open and liberation is attained.
Curses and various poisonous herbs,
Intended to harm one's body,
Mindful of that power of Avalokiteśvara,
They return upon the perpetrator.
Or if encountering evil rākṣasas,
Poisonous Nagas, and ghosts,
Mindful of that power of Avalokiteśvara,
At that moment none dare inflict harm.
If surrounded by evil beasts,
With terrifying sharp fangs and claws,
Mindful of that power of Avalokiteśvara,
They flee swiftly in boundless directions.
Vipers, adders, and scorpions,
Breathing poisonous vapor like blazing fires,
Mindful of that power of Avalokiteśvara,
Accordant with the sound, they turn away.
Clouds thunder and lightning flashes,
Hail falls and great rain pours,
Mindful of that power of Avalokiteśvara,
In due time they disperse.
When wandering beings are beset by peril,
Immeasurable sufferings pressing upon their bodies,
Avalokiteśvara's wondrous power of wisdom,
Can rescue the suffering of the world.
Endowed with spiritual powers,
Extensively cultivating wisdom and expedient means,
In lands of the ten directions,
There is no realm where he does not manifest.
Various evil destinies,
Hells, ghosts, and animals,
The suffering of birth, old age, sickness, and death,
Gradually are all caused to cease.
Genuine contemplation, pure contemplation,
Vast wisdom contemplation,
Compassionate contemplation, and loving contemplation,
Constantly prayed for, constantly looked up to!
Pure light without defilement,
The sun of wisdom dispels all darkness,
Able to subdue calamities of wind and fire,
Universally illuminating the world.
The body of compassion is thunderous like precepts,
The mind of love is a wondrous great cloud,
Raining down the amṛta Dharma rain,
Extinguishing the flames of afflictions.
In lawsuits before officials,
And amidst terrifying battle arrays,
Mindful of that power of Avalokiteśvara,
All hostilities disperse and retreat.
Wondrous sound, Avalokiteśvara,
Brahma-sound, ocean tide sound,
Surpassing worldly sounds;
Therefore one must constantly be mindful!
Thought after thought, do not give rise to doubt;
Avalokiteśvara, the pure sage,
In suffering, affliction, and the peril of death,
Can serve as a refuge.
Endowed with all virtues,
Beholding wandering beings with eyes of compassion,
An ocean of accumulated merit without measure;
Therefore one should bow down in homage!
At that time, Dharaṇīṃdhara Bodhisattva arose from his seat, went forward, and addressed the Buddha: "World-Honored One! If there are wandering beings who hear this chapter of Bodhisattva Avalokiteśvara, his sovereign deeds and spiritual powers of universal gate manifestation, you should know that this person's merit is not small."
When the Buddha expounded this Universal Gate Chapter, eighty-four thousand wandering beings in the assembly all generated the mind of anuttarasamyaksambodhi, which is beyond compare.
