At that time, the Bodhisattva Mahāsattvas who had arrived from other lands, exceeding the sands of eight Ganges rivers, arose in the great assembly, joined their palms, bowed, and addressed the Buddha:
"World-Honored One! If you permit us, after the Buddha's extinction, in this Sahā world, we will exert diligence and vigor, protecting, upholding, reading, reciting, copying, and making offerings to this scripture, and will widely expound it in this land."
Then the Buddha addressed the assembly of Bodhisattva Mahāsattvas: "Cease, Good children! There is no need for you to protect and uphold this scripture. For what reason? In my Sahā world, there are already Bodhisattva Mahāsattvas equal to the sands of sixty thousand Ganges rivers, each Bodhisattva having retinues equal to the sands of sixty thousand Ganges rivers. These persons will be able, after my extinction, to protect, uphold, read, recite, and widely expound this scripture."
When the Buddha spoke this, the ground of the great three-thousand-fold worlds of the Sahā world shook and cracked open, and from within it, immeasurable thousands of billions of Bodhisattva Mahāsattvas simultaneously welled forth.
All these Bodhisattvas had golden bodies, thirty-two marks, and immeasurable light; they had previously dwelled below this Sahā world, residing in open space within this realm. These Bodhisattvas, hearing the sound spoken by Śākyamuni Buddha, came forth from below.
Each Bodhisattva was a leader of a great assembly, each leading retinues equal to the sands of sixty thousand Ganges rivers; how much more so those who led retinues equal to fifty thousand, forty thousand, thirty thousand, twenty thousand, or ten thousand Ganges sands; how much more so those with retinues down to one Ganges sand, half a Ganges sand, a quarter, or down to one part in tens of billions of inconceivable numbers; how much more so those with retinues of tens of billions of inconceivable numbers; how much more so those with billions of retinues; how much more so those with tens of millions, millions, or down to ten thousand; how much more so those with one thousand, one hundred, or down to ten; how much more so those who led five, four, three, two, or one disciple; how much more so those who were solitary, delighting in isolated practice! Comparisons such as these are immeasurable, boundless, beyond calculation or parables to know.
These Bodhisattvas, having emerged from the earth, each went up into open space to the wonderful seven-jeweled stūpa, to where Prabhūtaratna Tathāgata and Śākyamuni Buddha were. Having arrived, they bowed with heads touching the feet of the two World-Honored Ones; they also went to the Buddhas seated upon lion seats beneath the jewel trees, bowed, circumambulated them three times to the right, joined their palms reverently, and praised them with various Bodhisattva praises, then stood on one side, joyfully gazing upon the two World-Honored Ones.
From the time these Bodhisattva Mahāsattvas first welled forth, praising the Buddhas with various Bodhisattva praises, a span of fifty minor kalpas elapsed. During this time, Śākyamuni Buddha sat in silence, and the fourfold assembly also sat in silence for fifty minor kalpas; through the Buddha's spiritual power, the great assembly perceived it as half a day.
At that time, the fourfold assembly, also through the Buddha's spiritual power, saw the Bodhisattvas filling the open space of immeasurable hundreds of thousands of millions of lands. Among this assembly of Bodhisattvas, there were four guides: the first named Viśiṣṭacāritra, the second named Anantacāritra, the third named Viśuddhacāritra, and the fourth named Supratiṣṭhitacāritra.
These four Bodhisattvas were the foremost leaders and guiding teachers among the multitude. Before the great assembly, each joined palms, observed Śākyamuni Buddha, and inquired: "World-Honored One! Are you free of sickness and affliction, practicing in peace and joy? Are those to be liberated easy to teach? They do not cause the World-Honored One to grow weary, do they?"
Then the four great Bodhisattvas spoke gāthās:
Is the World-Honored One peaceful and joyful,
Free of sickness and affliction?
In teaching and transforming wandering beings,
Are you free from weariness?
And are all the wandering beings
Easy to transform?
They do not cause the World-Honored One
To give rise to fatigue, do they?
Then the World-Honored One, amidst the great assembly of Bodhisattvas, spoke these words: "Thus it is, thus it is, Good children! The Tathāgata is peaceful and joyful, free of sickness and affliction. Wandering beings are easy to transform, and I have no fatigue.
For what reason? These wandering beings, life after life, have constantly received my transformation, and have also made offerings and shown reverence under past Buddhas, planting roots of virtue. When these wandering beings first saw my body and heard what I expounded, they all immediately believed, accepted, and entered the Tathāgata's wisdom—except for those who previously practiced and studied the Small Vehicle. Such persons as these, I now also cause to hear this scripture and enter the Buddha's wisdom."
Then the great Bodhisattvas spoke gāthās:
Excellent, excellent!
Great Hero, World-Honored One!
All these wandering beings
Are easy to transform.
Able to inquire into the Buddhas'
Profound wisdom,
Hearing it, they believe and practice;
We rejoice in accord!
Then the World-Honored One praised the leading great Bodhisattvas: "Excellent, excellent, Good children! You are able to give rise to a mind of rejoicing in accord with the Tathāgata."
At that time, Maitreya Bodhisattva and the assembly of Bodhisattvas equal to the sands of eight Ganges rivers all had this thought: "From the past until now, we have never seen or heard of such an assembly of great Bodhisattva Mahāsattvas welling forth from the earth, standing before the World-Honored One, joining palms, making offerings, and inquiring after the Tathāgata."
Then Maitreya Bodhisattva Mahāsattva, knowing the thoughts in the minds of the Bodhisattvas equal to the sands of eight Ganges rivers, and wishing to resolve his own doubt, joined his palms toward the Buddha and inquired in gāthās:
Immeasurable thousands of billions,
A great assembly of Bodhisattvas,
Never seen in the past;
We pray the Honored Among Two-Legged Beings will speak:
From where have they come?
By what causal condition have they assembled?
Huge bodies, great spiritual powers,
Wisdom inconceivable,
Their resolve and mindfulness firm,
Possessing great power of patience,
Delighted in by wandering beings—
From where have they come?
Each of these Bodhisattvas,
Leading retinues,
Their numbers having no measure,
Like the sands of the Ganges.
Some great Bodhisattvas,
Lead sixty thousand Ganges sands;
Such great multitudes as these,
Single-mindedly seek the Buddha path.
These great masters,
Sixty thousand Ganges sands,
Have come together to make offerings to the Buddha,
And to protect and uphold this scripture.
Those leading fifty thousand Ganges sands,
Their numbers exceed even these.
Forty thousand and thirty thousand,
Twenty thousand down to ten thousand,
One thousand, one hundred,
Down to one Ganges sand,
Half, a third, or a quarter,
Down to one part in tens of billions,
Tens of billions of inconceivable numbers,
Tens of billions of disciples,
Down to half a billion,
Their numbers surpass even these.
From one million to ten thousand,
One thousand and one hundred,
Fifty and ten,
Down to three, two, or one,
Solitary without retinues,
Delighting in solitary living,
Coming together to where the Buddha is,
Their numbers exceed even these.
Such great assemblies as these,
If someone were to count them with tally rods,
Throughout kalpas like Ganges sands,
They still could not fully know them.
All these great, majestic,
Vigorous Bodhisattva assemblies—
Who expounded the Dharma for them,
Teaching, transforming, and accomplishing them?
From whom did they first generate the resolve?
What Buddha-Dharma do they praise?
Whose scriptures do they uphold and practice?
What Buddha path do they cultivate?
Such Bodhisattvas as these,
With spiritual powers and great wisdom strength,
When the earth in the four directions cracked open,
All welled forth from within it.
World-Honored One, from the past,
I have never seen such a thing;
Pray speak of where they come from,
And the names of their lands.
I constantly travel throughout lands,
Yet have never seen this assembly;
Among this assembly,
I do not recognize a single person;
Suddenly emerging from the earth,
Pray speak of their causal conditions.
Now in this great assembly,
Immeasurable hundreds of thousands of billions,
All these Bodhisattvas,
Wish to understand this matter.
Regarding these Bodhisattva assemblies,
The root and branch causal conditions,
World-Honored One of immeasurable virtue,
We only pray you will resolve the assembly's doubts!
At that time, the emanation Buddhas of Śākyamuni Buddha, arriving from immeasurable thousands of billions of lands in other directions, sat cross-legged upon lion seats beneath jewel trees in the eight directions.
The attendants of those Buddhas each saw this great assembly of Bodhisattvas in the four directions of the great three-thousand-fold worlds, welling forth from the earth and residing in open space. Each addressed their Buddha: "World-Honored One! From where has this immeasurable, boundless incalculable assembly of Bodhisattvas come?"
Then the Buddhas each addressed their attendants: "Good children! Wait an instant. There is a Bodhisattva Mahāsattva named Maitreya, upon whom Śākyamuni Buddha conferred a prophecy that he will next become a Buddha. He has already inquired of this matter; the Buddha is now answering it, and through this you will hear it for yourselves."
Then Śākyamuni Buddha addressed Maitreya Bodhisattva: "Excellent, excellent, Ajita! That you are able to question the Buddha on such a great matter. You should all join together with one mind, put on the armor of vigor, and generate a firm resolve; the Tathāgata now wishes to reveal and proclaim the wisdom of the Buddhas, the sovereign spiritual powers of the Buddhas, the lion-like swift stride of the Buddhas, and the majestic, fierce, great power of the Buddhas."
Thereupon, the World-Honored One, wishing to restate this meaning, spoke gāthās:
Be vigorous with one mind;
I wish to speak of this matter.
Do not harbor doubt or regret;
The Buddha's wisdom is inconceivable.
Now bring forth the power of faith,
Abiding in patient goodness;
Dharmas never heard in the past,
You will now hear them all.
I now comfort you;
Do not harbor doubt or fear.
The Buddha has no ungenuine words;
His wisdom is immeasurable.
The foremost Dharma attained,
Is profound and beyond discrimination;
Such as this I will now expound;
Listen with one mind!
The World-Honored One, having spoken these gāthās, addressed Maitreya Bodhisattva: "I now announce to you all in this great assembly. Ajita! These great Bodhisattva Mahāsattvas, immeasurable, countless and incalculable, who welled forth from the earth, whom you have never seen in the past—after I attained anuttarasamyaksambodhi in this Sahā world, I taught, transformed, and guided these Bodhisattvas, subdued their minds, and caused them to generate the resolve for the path.
All these Bodhisattvas dwell below this Sahā world, residing in open space within this realm. Regarding all scriptures, they read, recite, penetrate fluently, contemplate, distinguish, and properly remember them.
Ajita! These Good children do not delight in speaking much amidst multitudes; they constantly delight in quiet places, diligently practicing vigor without resting; neither do they dwell relying on humans or devas. Constantly delighting in deep wisdom, they are free of hindrances, and constantly delight in the Dharma of the Buddhas, single-mindedly vigorous, seeking unexcelled wisdom."
Thereupon, the World-Honored One, wishing to restate this meaning, spoke gāthās:
Ajita, you should know!
All these great Bodhisattvas,
Throughout countless kalpas,
Have cultivated the Buddha's wisdom;
All were transformed by me,
Causing them to generate the great path-mind.
These are my children,
Relying upon this world,
Constantly practicing dhūta austerities,
Their resolve delighting in quiet places,
Abandoning the bustling commotion of crowds,
Not delighting in speaking much.
Children such as these,
Learn the Dharma of my path,
Day and night constantly vigorous,
For the sake of seeking the Buddha path,
Dwelling in the space below
This Sahā world.
Their power of resolve and mindfulness is firm,
Constantly and diligently seeking wisdom,
Expounding various wondrous dharmas,
Their minds are free of fear.
In the city of Gayā,
Seated beneath the Bodhi tree,
I attained the most right awakening,
And turned the unexcelled wheel of Dharma.
Only then did I teach and transform them,
Causing them first to generate the path-mind;
Now they all abide in non-retrogression,
And will all attain Buddhahood.
I now speak genuine words;
Believe with one mind:
From the distant past onward,
I have taught and transformed these multitudes!
At that time, Maitreya Bodhisattva Mahāsattva and the countless Bodhisattvas gave rise to doubts in their hearts, wondering at what had never occurred before, thinking: "How could the World-Honored One, in a short span of time, teach and transform such immeasurable, boundless incalculable great Bodhisattvas, causing them to abide in anuttarasamyaksambodhi?"
He immediately addressed the Buddha: "World-Honored One! When the Tathāgata was a prince, you left the Śākya palace, went not far from the city of Gayā, sat at the place of awakening, and accomplished anuttarasamyaksambodhi. From then until now, barely forty-odd years have passed.
World-Honored One! How could you, in this short time, perform such great Buddha activities, and through the Buddha's power and virtue teach and transform such an immeasurable assembly of great Bodhisattvas who are to accomplish anuttarasamyaksambodhi?
World-Honored One! If someone were to count this assembly of great Bodhisattvas throughout thousands of billions of kalpas, they could not exhaust them or reach their limits. These have, from the distant past onward, planted roots of virtue under immeasurable, boundless Buddhas, accomplished the Bodhisattva path, and constantly cultivated Brahma conduct.
World-Honored One! Such a matter as this is difficult for the world to believe. Suppose a man of handsome complexion and black hair, twenty-five years of age, points to a hundred-year-old man, saying: 'This is my son'; and that hundred-year-old man also points to the youth, saying: 'This is my father who begot and raised me.' This matter would be difficult to believe.
The Buddha is also like this: since attaining the path, in reality not long has passed, yet this great assembly of Bodhisattvas, throughout immeasurable thousands of billions of kalpas, for the sake of the Buddha path, have diligently exerted vigor, skillfully entering, exiting, and dwelling in immeasurable hundreds of thousands of millions of samādhis, attaining great spiritual powers, long cultivating Brahma conduct, skillfully and successively practicing all wholesome dharmas, adept in questions and answers, jewels among humans, exceedingly rare in all the world.
Today the World-Honored One only now states: 'When I attained the Buddha path, I first caused them to generate the resolve, teaching, transforming, and guiding them toward anuttarasamyaksambodhi.' It has not been long since the World-Honored One attained Buddhahood, yet you have accomplished this great work of virtue.
Although we believe what the Buddha expounds according to what is appropriate, that the words uttered by the Buddha are never false, and that all the Buddha knows is thoroughly penetrated; yet newly resolved Bodhisattvas, after the Buddha's extinction, if they hear these words, may not believe or receive them, giving rise to causal conditions of sinful karma that break the Dharma.
Yes, World-Honored One! Pray explain it for us to eliminate our doubts, so that Good children of future ages, hearing of this matter, will also not give rise to doubts."
Thereupon, Maitreya Bodhisattva, wishing to restate this meaning, spoke gāthās:
In the past, from the Śākya clan,
The Buddha left home near Gayā,
Sitting beneath the Bodhi tree;
Since then, not long has elapsed.
All these Buddha-children,
Their numbers cannot be measured;
Long having practiced the Buddha path,
Abiding in spiritual powers,
Skillfully learning the Bodhisattva path,
Unstained by worldly dharmas,
Like lotus flowers in water,
Welling forth from the earth.
All giving rise to reverent minds,
Standing before the World-Honored One.
This matter is difficult to conceive;
How can it be believed?
The Buddha's attainment of the path is very recent,
Yet what he has accomplished is exceedingly vast;
Pray eliminate the assembly's doubts,
Distinguishing it as it truly is.
Just as a vigorous young man,
Barely twenty-five years of age,
Shows people a hundred-year-old son,
With white hair and wrinkled face:
"This is my own flesh and blood";
And the son also says he is the father.
The father is young while the son is old;
The whole world would not believe it.
The World-Honored One is also like this;
Since attaining the path, it is very recent.
All these Bodhisattvas,
Firm in resolve, free of timidity,
Throughout immeasurable kalpas,
Have practiced the Bodhisattva path;
Skillful in answering difficult questions,
Their minds free of fear;
Resolute in their minds of patience,
Upright and possessing majesty,
Praised by Buddhas of the ten directions.
Skillfully able to distinguish and explain,
Not delighting in being among crowds,
Constantly favoring dhyāna meditation,
For the sake of seeking the Buddha path,
Dwelling in the space below.
We, hearing it from the Buddha,
Have no doubt in this matter;
May the Buddha, for future ages,
Proclaim it to bring understanding.
If there are those who regarding this scripture,
Give rise to doubt and do not believe,
They will immediately fall into evil destinies.
Pray explain it now:
These immeasurable Bodhisattvas,
How in a short span of time,
Did you teach them, cause them to generate the resolve,
And abide on the ground of non-retrogression?
