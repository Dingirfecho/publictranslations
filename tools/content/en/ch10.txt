At that time, the World-Honored One, on account of Bhaiṣajyarāja Bodhisattva, addressed eighty thousand great beings: "Bhaiṣajyarāja! Do you see in this great assembly the immeasurable devas, Naga kings, yakṣas, gandharvas, asuras, garuḍas, kiṃnaras, mahoragas, humans, non-humans, bhikṣus, bhikṣuṇīs, upāsakas, and upāsikās, those seeking to be Śrāvakas, those seeking to be Conditioned Buddhas, and those seeking the Buddha path? If such classes as these, before the Buddha, hear a single gāthā or a single phrase of the Lotus Sūtra of the Wonderful Dharma, or even for a single thought give rise to joy in accord, I confer prophecies upon them all: they will attain anuttarasamyaksambodhi."
The Buddha addressed Bhaiṣajyarāja: "Furthermore, after the Tathāgata's extinction, if there is a person who hears the Lotus Sūtra of the Wonderful Dharma, down to a single gāthā or a single phrase, and for a single thought gives rise to joy in accord, I also confer upon them the prophecy of anuttarasamyaksambodhi.
If there is a person who receives, upholds, reads, recites, explains, or copies the Lotus Sūtra of the Wonderful Dharma, down to a single gāthā, revering this scripture as they would the Buddha, making various offerings—flowers, incense, necklaces, powdered incense, paste incense, burning incense, silk canopies, banners, streamers, clothing, music, or even joining their palms in reverence: Bhaiṣajyarāja! You should know that these persons have already made offerings to ten billion Buddhas, fulfilling their great vows under those Buddhas, and out of compassion for wandering beings, have been born into this human realm.
Bhaiṣajyarāja! If someone asks: 'What kind of wandering beings in a future age will become Buddhas?' you should show them: 'These persons in a future age will certainly become Buddhas.'
For what reason? If a good man or good woman, regarding the Lotus Sūtra, down to a single phrase, receives, upholds, reads, recites, explains, or copies it, making various offerings to the scripture—flowers, incense, necklaces, powdered incense, paste incense, burning incense, silk canopies, banners, streamers, clothing, music, joining palms in reverence—this person should be looked up to and revered by all the world, and should be offered worship as one would make offerings to the Tathāgata. You should know that this person is a great Bodhisattva who has accomplished anuttarasamyaksambodhi, and out of compassion for wandering beings has vowed to be born here, widely expounding and distinguishing the Lotus Sūtra of the Wonderful Dharma. How much more so those who can fully receive, uphold, and make various offerings to it!
Bhaiṣajyarāja! You should know that this person voluntarily abandons their pure karmic retribution, and after my extinction, out of compassion for wandering beings, is born in an evil world to widely expound this scripture.
If this good man or good woman, after my extinction, can secretly expound the Lotus Sūtra for even a single person, down to a single phrase, you should know that this person is an envoy of the Tathāgata, dispatched by the Tathāgata, carrying out the activities of the Tathāgata. How much more so those who expound it widely amidst the great assembly!
Bhaiṣajyarāja! If an evil person, with an unwholesome mind, throughout an entire kalpa appears before the Buddha and constantly reviles the Buddha, their transgression is still light; but if a person, with a single evil word, defames householders or renouncers who read and recite the Lotus Sūtra, their transgression is exceedingly heavy.
Bhaiṣajyarāja! Those who read and recite the Lotus Sūtra—you should know that these persons adorn themselves with the adornments of the Buddha, and are carried upon the shoulders of the Tathāgata. Wherever they go, one should bow toward them, single-mindedly joining palms, reverently making offerings, honoring and praising them, with flowers, incense, necklaces, powdered incense, paste incense, burning incense, silk canopies, banners, streamers, clothing, delicacies, music, making the supreme offerings among humans to worship them, scattering heavenly jewels over them, and presenting heaps of heavenly treasures.
For what reason? Because when this person joyfully expounds the Dharma, if one hears it for an instant, one immediately attains ultimate anuttarasamyaksambodhi."
Thereupon, the World-Honored One, wishing to restate this meaning, spoke gāthās:
If one wishes to abide in the Buddha path,
And accomplish spontaneous wisdom,
One should constantly and diligently make offerings
To those who uphold the Lotus.
Those who wish quickly to attain
Wisdom of all kinds,
Should receive and uphold this scripture,
And make offerings to those who uphold it.
If there are those who can receive and uphold
The Lotus Sūtra of the Wonderful Dharma,
Know that they are envoys of the Buddha,
Pitying all wandering beings.
All who are able to receive and uphold
The Lotus Sūtra of the Wonderful Dharma,
Abandon pure lands,
And out of pity for beings are born here.
You should know that such persons as these,
Sovereign in where they wish to be born,
Are able within this evil world
Widely to expound the unexcelled Dharma.
One should use heavenly flowers and incense,
Heavenly precious clothing,
And heaps of heavenly subtle treasures,
To make offerings to the expounder of Dharma.
In the evil world after my extinction,
To those who can uphold this scripture,
One should join palms and bow reverently,
As if making offerings to the World-Honored One.
Superior delicacies of great sweetness,
And various garments,
Offer to these Buddha-children,
Hoping to hear it for an instant.
Those who can in future ages
Receive and uphold this scripture,
I send into the midst of humans,
To carry out the activities of the Tathāgata.
If throughout a kalpa,
One constantly harbors an unwholesome mind,
Grimacing and reviling the Buddha,
One incurs immeasurable heavy transgressions;
Yet for those who read, recite, and uphold
This Lotus Sūtra,
If one directs evil words at them for an instant,
That transgression surpasses the former.
If someone seeking the Buddha path,
Throughout an entire kalpa,
Joins palms before me,
Praising with countless verses;
Because of this praise of the Buddha,
They attain immeasurable merit;
Yet praising those who uphold this scripture,
That merit surpasses the former.
Throughout eighty billion kalpas,
With the most wonderful sights and sounds,
Scents, tastes, and tangibles,
Making offerings to those who uphold the scripture;
Having made such offerings,
If one attains hearing it for an instant,
One should celebrate within oneself:
"I have now attained great benefit!"
Bhaiṣajyarāja, I now tell you:
Among all the scriptures I have expounded,
Within this scripture,
The Lotus is foremost!
Then the Buddha further addressed Bhaiṣajyarāja Bodhisattva Mahāsattva: "The scriptures I have expounded are immeasurable thousands of billions—already expounded, presently expounded, and to be expounded—yet among them, this Lotus Sūtra is the most difficult to believe and difficult to understand.
Bhaiṣajyarāja! This scripture is the secret essential treasury of all Buddhas, not to be recklessly distributed or bestowed upon people. Guarded by the Buddhas, the World-Honored Ones, from the past until now it has never been openly expounded. And regarding this scripture, even while the Tathāgata is present, there is much resentment and envy; how much more so after his extinction?
Bhaiṣajyarāja! You should know that after the Tathāgata's extinction, those who can copy, uphold, read, recite, make offerings to, and expound it for others will be covered by the Tathāgata's robe, and guarded in the mindfulness of present Buddhas of other directions. These persons possess great power of faith, power of resolve, and power of wholesome roots. You should know that these persons dwell together with the Tathāgata, and their heads are rubbed by the hand of the Tathāgata.
Bhaiṣajyarāja! In every place, whether expounded, read, recited, copied, or wherever copies of the scripture reside, one should erect a seven-jeweled stūpa, making it lofty, broad, and adorned; there is no need to place relics within it.
For what reason? Within it is already the complete body of the Tathāgata. To this stūpa, one should offer all flowers, incense, necklaces, silk canopies, banners, streamers, music, and hymns, making offerings, revering, honoring, and praising it. If people see this stūpa, bow down, and make offerings, you should know that they are all near to anuttarasamyaksambodhi.
Bhaiṣajyarāja! Many people, householders and renouncers, practice the Bodhisattva path; if they cannot see, hear, read, recite, copy, uphold, and make offerings to this Lotus Sūtra, you should know that these persons do not yet skillfully practice the Bodhisattva path. If they hear this scripture, they are able skillfully to practice the Bodhisattva path.
If there are wandering beings seeking the Buddha path who see or hear this Lotus Sūtra, and having heard it, believe, understand, receive, and uphold it, you should know that these persons draw near to anuttarasamyaksambodhi.
Bhaiṣajyarāja! Suppose a man is thirsty and in need of water, digging on an arid high plain to seek it. Still seeing dry earth, he knows that water is yet distant; applying his labor without ceasing, he progressively sees damp earth, and gradually reaches mud; his mind becomes certain: he knows that water must be near.
Bodhisattvas are also like this: if they have not heard, not understood, and not been able to cultivate this Lotus Sūtra, you should know that these persons are still far from anuttarasamyaksambodhi; if they hear, understand, contemplate, and cultivate it, they must know that they are near to anuttarasamyaksambodhi.
For what reason? All Bodhisattvas' anuttarasamyaksambodhi belongs entirely to this scripture; this scripture opens the gate of expedient means and reveals the genuine mark. The treasury of this Lotus Sūtra is deep, firm, and remote, where none can reach; now the Buddha, teaching and accomplishing Bodhisattvas, opens and reveals it.
Bhaiṣajyarāja! If there are Bodhisattvas who, hearing this Lotus Sūtra, are startled, doubtful, and fearful, you should know that they are newly resolved Bodhisattvas; if Śrāvakas hearing this scripture are startled, doubtful, and fearful, you should know that they are persons of arrogant pride.
Bhaiṣajyarāja! If there are good men and good women who, after the Tathāgata's extinction, wish to expound this Lotus Sūtra for the fourfold assembly, how should they expound it?
These good men and good women should enter the Tathāgata's room, put on the Tathāgata's robe, sit upon the Tathāgata's seat, and only then expound this scripture widely for the fourfold assembly.
The Tathāgata's room is the great compassionate mind toward all wandering beings; the Tathāgata's robe is the gentle and patient mind; the Tathāgata's seat is the emptiness of all dharmas. Dwelling peacefully within these, afterward with an unflagging mind, one should expound this Lotus Sūtra widely for Bodhisattvas and the fourfold assembly.
Bhaiṣajyarāja! In other lands, I will dispatch conjured persons to assemble audiences to hear the Dharma for them, and also dispatch conjured bhikṣus, bhikṣuṇīs, upāsakas, and upāsikās to listen to their expounding. These conjured persons, hearing the Dharma, will believe and accept it, following without opposition. If the expounder of Dharma is in a quiet, leisure place, I will then dispatch devas, Naga kings, spirits, gandharvas, asuras, and the like to listen to their expounding.
Although I am in another land, from time to time I will cause the expounder of Dharma to see my body. If they forget a phrase or verse of this scripture, I will speak it for them, causing them to be fully restored."
Thereupon, the World-Honored One, wishing to restate this meaning, spoke gāthās:
If one wishes to abandon all negligence,
One should listen to this scripture;
This scripture is difficult to hear,
And those who believe and accept it are also rare.
Like a person thirsty for water,
Digging upon an arid high plain,
Still seeing dry earth,
He knows water is still far away;
Gradually seeing damp earth and mud,
He knows with certainty that water is near.
Bhaiṣajyarāja, you should know!
Such persons as these,
Not hearing the Lotus Sūtra,
Are far removed from the Buddha's wisdom;
If they hear this profound scripture,
They definitively understand the Śrāvaka Dharma.
This is the King of all scriptures;
Having heard it and contemplated it carefully,
You should know that such persons
Are near to the Buddha's wisdom.
If a person expounds this scripture,
They should enter the Tathāgata's room,
Put on the Tathāgata's robe,
And sit upon the Tathāgata's seat;
Dwelling amidst the assembly without fear,
Widely distinguishing and expounding it.
Great compassion is the room,
Gentle patience is the robe,
The emptiness of all dharmas is the seat;
Dwelling here, one expounds the Dharma.
When expounding this scripture,
If someone reviles and abuses with evil mouths,
Or attacks with knives, sticks, tiles, and stones,
Mindful of the Buddha, one should endure it.
In my thousands of billions of lands,
I manifest pure, firm bodies,
Throughout immeasurable billions of kalpas,
Expounding the Dharma for wandering beings.
If after my extinction,
There are those who can expound this scripture,
I will dispatch conjured fourfold assemblies,
Bhikṣus and bhikṣuṇīs,
And pure faithful men and women,
To make offerings to the Dharma teacher,
Guiding wandering beings,
Assembling them to hear the Dharma.
If people wish to inflict harm,
With knives, sticks, tiles, and stones,
I will dispatch transformed persons
To serve as guards for them.
If the expounder of Dharma,
Is alone in a quiet, leisure place,
Silent without the sound of people,
Reading and reciting this scripture,
I will then manifest for them
A pure, radiant body.
If they forget a chapter or verse,
I will speak it to make them fluent.
If a person possesses these virtues,
Whether expounding for the fourfold assembly,
Or reading and reciting the scripture in an empty place,
They will all see my body.
If a person is in an empty, quiet place,
I will dispatch devas, Naga kings,
Yakṣas, ghosts, and spirits,
To serve as audiences listening to the Dharma.
This person delights in expounding the Dharma,
Distinguishing without hindrance;
Because of the protective mindfulness of the Buddhas,
They can cause the great assembly to rejoice.
If one draws close to the Dharma teacher,
One swiftly attains the Bodhisattva path;
Following and learning under this master,
One attains beholding Buddhas like Ganges sands.
