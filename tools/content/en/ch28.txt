At that time, Samantabhadra Bodhisattva, through sovereign spiritual powers, majestic virtue, and fame, together with an immeasurable, boundless, countless assembly of great Bodhisattvas, arrived from the eastern direction. The lands they passed through universally shook, raining jewel lotus flowers, and producing immeasurable hundreds of thousands of millions of varieties of music.
Furthermore, surrounded by an assembly of countless devas, Nagas, yakṣas, gandharvas, asuras, garuḍas, kiṃnaras, mahoragas, humans, and non-humans, each displaying majestic virtue and spiritual powers, he arrived at Mount Gṛdhrakūṭa in the Sahā world. Bowing with his head touching the feet of Śākyamuni Buddha, he circumambulated him seven times to the right, and addressed the Buddha:
"World-Honored One! In the land of Ratnatejobhyudgatarāja Buddha, from afar I heard the Lotus Sūtra being expounded in this Sahā world, and together with an immeasurable, boundless assembly of hundreds of thousands of millions of Bodhisattvas have come to listen and receive it. We only pray that the World-Honored One will expound it for us. If good men and good women, after the Tathāgata's extinction, wish to attain this Lotus Sūtra, how can they attain it?"
The Buddha addressed Samantabhadra Bodhisattva: "If a good man or good woman accomplishes four dharmas, after the Tathāgata's extinction they will attain this Lotus Sūtra: first, being guarded in the mindfulness of the Buddhas; second, planting roots of virtue; third, entering the assembly of right determination; fourth, generating the mind to save all wandering beings. If a good man or good woman accomplishes these four dharmas, after the Tathāgata's extinction they will certainly attain this scripture."
Then Samantabhadra Bodhisattva addressed the Buddha: "World-Honored One! In the final five hundred years of the turbid, evil world, if there are those who receive and uphold this scripture, I will guard and protect them, eliminate their misfortunes, cause them to attain peace and security, and ensure that none who seek their shortcomings gain an opening:
Whether Maras, Mara sons, Mara daughters, Mara subjects, those possessed by Maras, yakṣas, rākṣasas, kumbhāṇḍas, piśācas, kṛtyas, pūtanas, or vetālas—all who torment people will gain no opening.
If this person, walking or standing, reads and recites this scripture, I at that time, riding a six-tusked white elephant king together with an assembly of great Bodhisattvas, will go to where they are, manifest my body, make offerings, protect them, comfort their minds, and make offerings to the Lotus Sūtra.
If this person sits and contemplates this scripture, I will again ride the white elephant king and manifest before them. If that person forgets a single phrase or a single verse of the Lotus Sūtra, I will teach them, read and recite it together with them, and restore their fluency.
At that time, those who receive, uphold, read, and recite the Lotus Sūtra will behold my body, rejoice greatly, and redouble their vigor; because of seeing me, they will immediately attain samādhis and dhāraṇīs: named the Rotating dhāraṇī, the Hundreds of Thousands of Millions of Rotations dhāraṇī, and the Dharma Sound Expedient Means dhāraṇī—attaining dhāraṇīs such as these.
World-Honored One! If in future worlds during the final five hundred years of the turbid, evil world, bhikṣus, bhikṣuṇīs, upāsakas, or upāsikās who seek, receive, uphold, read, recite, and copy, wishing to cultivate this Lotus Sūtra, for three times seven days they should be single-mindedly vigorous.
When three times seven days are fulfilled, I will ride a six-tusked white elephant, surrounded by immeasurable Bodhisattvas, and in a body that all wandering beings delight in seeing, manifest before that person to expound the Dharma, instructing, benefiting, and delighting them, and will also bestow upon them a dhāraṇī mantra. Because of attaining this dhāraṇī, no non-human can destroy them, nor can they be seduced by women; I myself will also constantly protect this person. We only pray that the World-Honored One will permit me to speak this dhāraṇī mantra."
Immediately before the Buddha he spoke the mantra:
ADAṆḌE DAṆḌAPATI DAṆḌĀVARTANI DAṆḌAKUŚALE DAṆḌASUDHĀRI SUDHĀRI SUDHĀRAPATI BUDDHAPAŚYANE SARVADHĀRAṆĪ-ĀVARTANI SARVABHĀṢĀ-ĀVARTANI SU-ĀVARTANI SAṄGHAPARĪKṢAṆI SAṄGHANIRGHĀTANI ASAMPI SAṄGHAPAGATE TI-ADHVA-SAṄGATULYA-ARATE-PRĀPTE SARVASAṄGĀ-SAMATIKRĀNTE SARVADHARMA-SUPARĪKṢITE SARVASATTVARUTAKAUŚALYĀNUGATE SIṂHAVIKRĪḌITE SVĀHĀ
"World-Honored One! If there are Bodhisattvas who hear this dhāraṇī, they should know that it is the spiritual power of Samantabhadra. If the Lotus Sūtra travels through Jambudvīpa and there are those who uphold it, they should think: 'This is entirely through the majestic spiritual power of Samantabhadra.'
If someone receives, upholds, reads, recites, properly remembers, understands its meaning, and practices as taught, you should know that this person practices the conduct of Samantabhadra, has deeply planted roots of virtue under immeasurable, boundless Buddhas, and their head is rubbed by the hands of the Tathāgatas.
If they merely copy it, when this person's life ends, they will be reborn in the Trāyastriṃśa Heaven; at that time, eighty-four thousand heavenly maidens making music will come to welcome them, and that person, wearing a crown of the seven treasures, will delight in entertainment amidst the maidens; how much more so receiving, upholding, reading, reciting, properly remembering, understanding its meaning, and practicing as taught!
If someone receives, upholds, reads, recites, and understands its meaning, when their life ends, a thousand Buddhas will extend their hands, so that they will not fear nor fall into evil destinies, immediately proceeding to Tuṣita Heaven to where Maitreya Bodhisattva is. Maitreya Bodhisattva, endowed with the thirty-two marks, is surrounded by an assembly of great Bodhisattvas and has hundreds of thousands of millions of heavenly maidens as attendants; being born in their midst, they possess such merits and benefits as these.
Therefore, those who possess wisdom should single-mindedly copy it themselves or cause others to copy it, receive, uphold, read, recite, properly remember, and practice it as taught.
World-Honored One! I now, through spiritual powers, protect this scripture; after the Tathāgata's extinction, within Jambudvīpa I will cause it to be widely propagated, never allowing it to be cut off."
Then Śākyamuni Buddha praised: "Excellent, excellent, Samantabhadra! That you are able to protect and aid this scripture, bringing peace, joy, and benefit to many wandering beings. You have already accomplished inconceivable merits, deep and great compassion; from the distant past onward, you generated the resolve for anuttarasamyaksambodhi, and are able to make such vows of spiritual powers to protect this scripture. I, through spiritual powers, will protect those who can receive and uphold the name of Samantabhadra Bodhisattva.
Samantabhadra! If someone receives, upholds, reads, recites, properly remembers, cultivates, and copies this Lotus Sūtra, you should know that this person beholds Śākyamuni Buddha, as if hearing this scripture directly from the Buddha's mouth; you should know that this person makes offerings to Śākyamuni Buddha; you should know that for this person, the Buddha praises: 'Excellent!'; you should know that this person's head is rubbed by the hand of Śākyamuni Buddha; you should know that this person is covered by the robe of Śākyamuni Buddha.
Such a person as this will no longer be greedily attached to worldly pleasures, will not favor non-Buddhist scriptures or writings, and will not delight in drawing close to such people or evil persons—such as butchers, those who raise pigs, sheep, chickens, and dogs, hunters, or those who peddle female charms.
This person's mind is upright and direct, with proper mindfulness, possessing the power of merit and virtue. This person is not tormented by the three poisons, nor tormented by envy, self-grasping pride, perverted pride, or arrogant pride. This person has few desires, knows contentment, and is able to cultivate the practices of Samantabhadra.
Samantabhadra! After the Tathāgata's extinction, in the final five hundred years, if someone sees one who receives, upholds, reads, and recites the Lotus Sūtra, they should think: 'This person before long will proceed to the place of awakening, shatter the hosts of Maras, attain anuttarasamyaksambodhi, turn the wheel of Dharma, strike the Dharma drum, blow the Dharma conch, rain the Dharma rain, and sit upon the lion Dharma seat amidst the great assembly of devas and humans.'
Samantabhadra! If in future worlds someone receives, upholds, reads, and recites this scripture, this person will no longer crave clothing, bedding, food, drink, or livelihoods; what they wish will not be in vain, and in this present life they will attain karmic retributions of blessing.
If someone slights and defames them, saying: 'You are a mad person, vainly performing these practices, ultimately obtaining nothing'; for such sinful retribution, life after life they will have no eyes.
If someone makes offerings and praises them, in this present life they will attain present visible fruits.
If someone sees an upholder of this scripture and proclaims their faults and evils, whether real or unreal, this person in this present life will contract white leprosy.
If someone derisively laughs at them, life after life their teeth will be sparse and missing, with ugly lips, flat nose, twisted limbs, squinting eyes, a stinking body, covered in foul sores, pus, blood, dropsy, and shortness of breath, suffering from various severe illnesses.
Therefore, Samantabhadra! If you see someone who upholds this scripture, you should rise and welcome them from afar, showing reverence as if to the Buddha!"
When this Encouragement of Bodhisattva Samantabhadra Chapter was expounded, Bodhisattvas equal to the sands of the Ganges attained hundreds of thousands of millions of rotating dhāraṇīs; Bodhisattvas equal to the dust motes of a great three-thousand-fold worlds fully perfected the path of Samantabhadra.
When the Buddha expounded this scripture, Samantabhadra and the Bodhisattvas, Śāriputra and the Śrāvakas, as well as devas, Nagas, humans, and non-humans, the entire great assembly, rejoiced greatly, received and upheld the Buddha's words, bowed in reverence, and departed.
This is the Sūtra of the Lotus Flower of the Wonderful Dharma.
