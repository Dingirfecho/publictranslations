At that time, Bhaiṣajyarāja Bodhisattva Mahāsattva and Mahāpratibhāna Bodhisattva Mahāsattva, together with a retinue of twenty thousand Bodhisattvas, made this vow before the Buddha:
"We only pray that the World-Honored One will have no anxiety. After the Buddha's extinction, we will uphold, read, recite, and expound this scripture. Wandering beings of the future evil age will have fewer wholesome roots, much arrogant pride, greed for gain and offerings, increasing unwholesome roots, and will be far removed from liberation. Although they are difficult to teach, we will give rise to great power of patience, reading, reciting, holding, expounding, copying, and making various offerings to this scripture, not begrudging physical life."
Then five hundred arhats in the assembly who had received prophecies addressed the Buddha: "World-Honored One! We too make our own vows: in other lands, we will widely expound this scripture."
Furthermore, eight thousand practitioners in training and beyond training who had received prophecies arose from their seats, joined their palms toward the Buddha, and made this vow:
"World-Honored One! We too will widely expound this scripture in other lands. For what reason? In this Sahā world, people are full of flaws and evils, harboring arrogant pride, shallow in virtue, angry, turbid, flattering, and crooked, with ungenuine minds."
At that time, the Buddha's maternal aunt, the bhikṣuṇī Mahāprajāpatī, together with six thousand bhikṣuṇīs in training and beyond training, arose from their seats, single-mindedly joined their palms, and gazed upon the venerable countenance without glancing away for an instant.
Then the World-Honored One addressed Gautamī: "Why do you look upon the Tathāgata with a sorrowful expression? Does your mind think that I have not spoken your name to bestow a prophecy of anuttarasamyaksambodhi?
Gautamī! I previously stated generally that all Śrāvakas have already received prophecies. Now if you wish to know your prophecy: in future worlds, within the Dharma of sixty-eight billion Buddhas, you will be a great Dharma teacher, and the six thousand bhikṣuṇīs in training and beyond training will all be Dharma teachers together. In this way, gradually perfecting the Bodhisattva path, you will become a Buddha named Sarvasattvapriyadarśana Tathāgata, Arhat, Samyaksambuddha, Vidyācaraṇasampanna, Sugata, Lokavid, Anuttara, Puruṣadamyasārathi, Śāstā Devamanuṣyāṇām, Buddha, Bhagavān.
Gautamī! This Sarvasattvapriyadarśana Buddha and the six thousand Bodhisattvas will successively bestow prophecies of attaining anuttarasamyaksambodhi."
Then Rāhula's mother, the bhikṣuṇī Yaśodharā, thought: "The World-Honored One, in bestowing prophecies, has uniquely omitted my name."
The Buddha addressed Yaśodharā: "You in future worlds, within the Dharma of hundreds of thousands of millions of Buddhas, will cultivate Bodhisattva conduct as a great Dharma teacher, gradually fulfilling the Buddha path. In the land Bhadra, you will become a Buddha named Daśasahasragauraprabhāsa Tathāgata, Arhat, Samyaksambuddha, Vidyācaraṇasampanna, Sugata, Lokavid, Anuttara, Puruṣadamyasārathi, Śāstā Devamanuṣyāṇām, Buddha, Bhagavān. That Buddha's lifespan will be immeasurable and incalculable kalpas."
Then the bhikṣuṇī Mahāprajāpatī and the bhikṣuṇī Yaśodharā, along with their retinues, all rejoiced greatly, attaining what they had never had before, and immediately spoke gāthās before the Buddha:
The World-Honored One, the Guide,
Brings peace to devas and humans,
We, hearing the prophecy,
Have minds completely at peace.
Having spoken these gāthās, the bhikṣuṇīs addressed the Buddha: "World-Honored One! We too are able widely to proclaim this scripture in lands of other directions."
At that time, the World-Honored One looked upon eighty billion inconceivable numbers of Bodhisattva Mahāsattvas. All these Bodhisattvas—who were avaivartika, turning the irreversible wheel of Dharma, and had attained dhāraṇīs—arose from their seats, went before the Buddha, single-mindedly joined their palms, and thought:
"If the World-Honored One commands us to uphold and expound this scripture, in accord with the Buddha's authoritative teaching we will widely proclaim this Dharma."
Again they thought: "The Buddha is now silent and does not issue a command; what should we do?"
Then the Bodhisattvas, reverently obeying the Buddha's intent and wishing to fulfill their own original vows, before the Buddha made the lion's roar and proclaimed a vow:
"World-Honored One! After the Tathāgata's extinction, traveling throughout worlds of the ten directions, we will cause wandering beings to copy this scripture, receive, uphold, read, recite, explain its meaning, practice it according to the Dharma, and remember it properly—all through the Buddha's majestic power. We only pray that the World-Honored One, dwelling in other directions, will gaze upon and protect us from afar!"
Immediately the Bodhisattvas simultaneously raised their voices, speaking gāthās:
We only pray you will have no anxiety;
After the Buddha's extinction,
In a terrifying, evil world,
We will widely expound it.
There are foolish people,
Abusing and reviling with evil mouths,
And even attacking with knives and sticks;
We will endure it all.
Bhikṣus in the evil world,
With perverse wisdom and crooked minds,
Claiming to have attained what they have not attained,
Filled with minds of self-grasping pride.
Or there are those in the araṇya,
Wearing patched robes in quiet leisure,
Claiming they practice the genuine path,
Despising people in the world.
Greedily attached to gain and offerings,
They expound the Dharma to white-robed laity,
Revered by the world,
As if arhats of the six spiritual superknowledges.
These people harbor evil minds,
Constantly thinking of worldly activities,
Taking the false name of araṇya,
Fond of pointing out our faults,
Speaking words like these:
"All these bhikṣus,
Greedy for gain and offerings,
Speak non-Buddhist treatises;
Composing this scripture themselves,
They deceive and confuse worldly people;
To seek fame and renown,
They distinguish in this scripture."
Constantly amidst the great assembly,
Wishing to defame us,
Addressing kings, ministers,
Brāhmaṇas, householders,
And other assemblies of bhikṣus,
Slandering and speaking evil of us,
Saying: "These are people of false views,
Speaking non-Buddhist treatises."
Because we revere the Buddha,
We will endure all these evils.
Mocked by them with words:
"You are all Buddhas!"
Derisive words such as these,
We will endure and accept them all.
In the turbid kalpa and evil world,
There are many terrors;
Evil ghosts will enter their bodies,
Reviling and insulting us.
We, revering and trusting the Buddha,
Will put on the armor of patience;
For the sake of expounding this scripture,
We will endure these difficult hardships.
We do not cherish physical life,
Caring only for the unexcelled path;
We in future ages,
Will protect what the Buddha entrusted;
The World-Honored One himself must know!
Evil bhikṣus of the turbid world,
Not knowing the Buddha's expedient means,
Expounding the Dharma according to what is appropriate,
With evil mouths and scowling faces,
Frequently expelling us,
Sending us far from stūpas and temples.
Evils such as these,
Mindful of the Buddha's authoritative teaching,
We will endure all these matters.
In all villages and cities,
Wherever there are those who seek the Dharma,
We will all go to where they are,
To expound the Dharma entrusted by the Buddha.
We are envoys of the World-Honored One,
Dwelling amidst the assembly without fear;
We will expound the Dharma skillfully;
May the Buddha dwell peacefully at ease!
Before the World-Honored One,
And the arriving Buddhas of the ten directions,
We make such vows as these;
The Buddha himself knows our hearts.
