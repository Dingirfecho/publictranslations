Part I

Supplication to the Eight Vidyadharas of the Noble Land

འཕགས་ཡུལ་རིག་འཛིན་བརྒྱད་ཀྱི་གསོལ་འདེབས།

The supplication to the eight Vidyadharas of the Noble Land:

From the sky Dhatu of the Dharmakaya endowed with the supreme of all aspects,

the full moon of the Sambhogakaya endowed with the seven branches,

for the assembly of those to be tamed, in the manner of inconceivable Nirmanakayas,

transforms into the net of magical illusion. To you I supplicate.

In the east, in the charnel ground of Cool Grove,

you opened the door of the secret treasury of Yangdak Mind,

and gained mastery over the wish-fulfilling treasure of the two siddhis.

Master Humkara and the others, I supplicate. In the south, in the charnel ground of Perfected in Body,

you planted well the seed of Manjushri’s Body,

and the fruit of the four vajras fully ripened.

At the feet of Manjushri Yama, I supplicate. In the west, in the charnel ground of Lanka Mound,

having thoroughly crossed the ocean of Padma Speech,

you obtained the wealth of knowledge of things as they are and in their multiplicity.

At the feet of Nagarjuna Garbha, I supplicate. In the north, in the charnel ground of Lotus Mound,

striking with the vajra weapon of Kila Activity,

you attained the power to liberate the three worlds into the Dhatu.

At the feet of Prabhahasti, I supplicate. In the southeast, in the charnel ground of Spontaneously Accomplished Mound,

constantly delighting in the joyous consort of Mamo Bötong,

the youth of undefiled great bliss fully flourished.

At the feet of Dhanasamskrita, I supplicate. In the southwest, in the charnel ground of Great Secret Display,

enjoying the essence of the amrita of qualities,

you arose in the supreme body of the rainbow body without transference.

At the feet of Vimalamitra, I supplicate. In the northwest, in the charnel ground of Pervasive Great Joy,

having rewarded the hosts of Worldly Offering and Praise,

you defeated the battle lines of the vicious hosts of Mara.

At the feet of Rombuguhya, I supplicate. In the northeast, in the charnel ground of World Mound,

with the mass of fire of Wrathful Mantra and Curses fiercely blazing,

you attained the power to burn adversaries like grass and trees.

At the feet of Shantigarbha, I supplicate. In all the buddha fields of the three kayas,

having attained manifest liberation in the eight sadhana classes,

you attained the supreme state of the four Vidyadharas.

To Orgyen Threngtsal, I supplicate. Having thus supplicated, may all

the blessings of the knowledge, love and power of the Victorious Ones, all glory and excellence,

be well obtained by me, ripening into the vajra body,

and may I become the glory of all wandering beings without exception.

This Supplication to the Eight Supreme Vidyadharas, called Continuous Rain of Blessings, arose spontaneously in the mind of the renunciant called Jigme Trinle Özer, a Dzogchen yogin who, having well received empowerment into the mandala of Secret Mantra, had not been entered by the obstacle of doubt, at the request of Rabjam Döndrub Gyatso of Riwo Ganden; having gained confidence, it was propagated from Tsongön in the north. May it become a cause for blessings to enter the mindstreams of all who have obtained a connection with the Guru.

Mangalam.

Part II

The Supplication to the Eight Great Siddhas of the Land of Tibet: The Great Cloud of Blessings.

བོད་ཡུལ་གྲུབ་ཆེན་བརྒྱད་ཀྱི་གསོལ་འདེབས་བྱིན་རླབས་སྤྲིན་ཆེན་བཞུགས་སོ། །

From the sky Dhatu of unchanging great bliss,

the cloud of the net of magical illusion, the complete Sambhogakaya of all aspects,

showers the rain of all that is desired upon those to be tamed pervading space.

To the assembly of Vidyadhara Gurus, I supplicate.

In the red rock cave of Chimphu at Samye,

in the great mandala of the Eight Authoritative Teachings, the practice of Secret Mantra,

you ripened the karmically destined king, lord and subjects.

To Orgyen Thötreng Tsal, I supplicate.

In Drak Yangdzong, practice place of the land of Tibet,

having manifestly accomplished Manjushri Yamantaka,

you struck down the transgressor and drove the kila into rock.

At the feet of Sangye Yeshe, I supplicate. In the supreme site of Chimphu, the glorious hermitage,

you fully accomplished the deities of Padma Speech,

and proclaimed the neigh of the horse that masters the three worlds.

At the feet of Gyalwa Chokyang, I supplicate. In Lhodrak Kharchu, in the glorious Phang cave,

the accomplishment of Yangdak Mind became manifest,

and the power of realized intention was perfected, with rays greater than the sun.

At the feet of Namkhai Nyingpo, I supplicate. At the glorious Utse of Samye of the three families,

having manifested the realized intention of Chemchok Qualities,

with unwavering samadhi you established the land of Tibet in the Dharma.

At the feet of the sovereign King, I supplicate. In the three places, Mönkha, Ne’uring and Senge Dzong,

you manifestly accomplished the deities of Kila Activity,

attained a miraculous body, and raised the corpses of the dead.

At the feet of Yeshe Tsogyal, I supplicate. In the snow enclosure of Yartong Shampo,

having beheld the faces of the deities of Mamo Bötong,

you subjugated the mamos of appearance and existence and put them to work.

At the feet of Palgyi Yeshe, I supplicate. In Paro Taktsang, in the Samdrub cave,

you accomplished well the mandala of Worldly Offering and Praise,

and, enslaving the arrogant gods and rakshasas, accomplished the four activities.

At the feet of Palgyi Senge, I supplicate. In Yamalung and in Changra of Khotan,

having fully accomplished the deities of Wrathful Mantra and Curses,

you possess the blazing fire of might and the eye of Wisdom.

At the feet of Vairochana, I supplicate. Having thus supplicated, may the blessings

of the Vidyadhara Gurus of the three times enter my heart.

Having obtained all the supreme and common siddhis,

may the great benefit of the teachings and of wandering beings be accomplished.

This too, at the innermost hermitage of the practice place known in the south as Karmo Taktsang, called Tashi Palbar Pema Deden Ling, was composed by Mipham Nampar Gyalwa himself while he was staying in the approach and accomplishment of the great accomplishment rite of the Eight Authoritative Teachings, on the twenty-first day of the Chotrul month of the new year of the Water Snake, Namgyal. May virtue and goodness increase.

Mangalam.
